Working as a lab

Lab setup

Turn databases on or off, and set your lab's name, time zone and dates.

Admins only

After the first survey, the lab's setup is in Settings, under Lab. Admins change it; everyone else sees it, read only, so they know how the lab is set up. Each change is saved as you make it.

  • General — the lab's name, time zone, dates and genotyping day.
  • Databases — switch each database and the calendar and notebook on or off; Other databases lists the ones made with Add database and members' own.
  • People & access — the lab's people (People and guests), its project groups, and What members may do: create their own databases, add ones for the whole lab, keep a copy of the lab, make tokens.
  • Statistics — the lab's animals in numbers, by person, how full each rack and box is, and what is coming up. Admins choose there who may change each rack, box and incubator.
  • History — the latest bulk changes, with Undo, and for admins the latest of every change (Undo and history).

Switching a database off hides it and closes it to members, even by a saved link; its data is kept, and admins can still open it (a note says it is off).

Under General, besides its name:

  • Time zone — what "today" and "overdue" mean, and the times shown. Blank uses the server's (or the computer's).
  • Dates — how dates are written in lists and messages: Sep 26, 2026, 26 Sep 2026 or 2026-09-26. Date fields you type into follow your computer's language.
  • Genotyping day — the day after birth the genotyping reminder falls on (P28 unless you change it).

Troubleshooting

A database disappeared from the sidebar

An admin may have switched it off in Settings → Databases; its data is kept, and it comes back when it is switched on.
