Your databases

Plants and field crops

Sowings, trays or plots, and the seed you harvest and sow again, a generation at a time.

Add database in the sidebar offers two starting points for plants. Plants is for pots and trays in growth rooms, growth chambers or a greenhouse, as an Arabidopsis, tobacco or rice lab keeps them. Field crops is for plots in a field, raised first in a nursery (a seedbed) and transplanted. Either one is sown and harvested rather than born and dead, and has no sex.

What it keeps

  • Plants — one row per plant, or per group of plants with a count. Sown is its sowing date, and Age is days after sowing; Harvested / removed is filled in when you set its status to harvested.
  • Trays (or Plots) — where the plants are, placed on the grid of a growth room, greenhouse or field. A field is a grid of plots, the way a rack is a grid of cages: give it rows and columns, and drag each plot into place.
  • Lines — each with its background (Col-0, a variety), source and stock number, selection marker, whether it is Transgenic and under which Biosafety permit, and its Construct: the plasmid it was transformed with, chosen from the Plasmids database and linked to it.
  • Sowings — a batch sown together, with its date, how many were sown, the seed lot it came from, its Generation (T2, F3) and its stage: seed, stratifying, seedling, transplanted, flowering, seed set and harvested, or in the nursery for a field crop. A sowing in the nursery can sit in the nursery (苗圃) as its place.
  • Crosses — the two parent lines, when it was pollinated, and when its seed was collected.
  • Environment — readings for each growth room or field: temperature, humidity, light and day length.

Seed lots

The Seeds tab lists every seed lot, those with seed left first. Each has its line and generation, what it was harvested from, how much was harvested and how much is left (in mg, g, seeds, tubes or packets), where it is kept, its germination and its selection plate.

  • Harvest seed — the wheat button on a plant, a sowing or a cross opens a new lot already filled in: its line, today's date, and its generation. Seed from a selfed plant is one generation on from the plant (a T1 plant gives T2 seed); seed from a cross is F1. Leave Mark what it was harvested from as harvested ticked and the plants are marked harvested and dated, and a cross is marked collected.
  • Sow — the seedling button on a lot starts a sowing from it, on the Sowings tab. The sowing takes the lot's line and generation, and what you type in Seed taken comes off what is left (in the lot's own unit). Counted in seeds, it also fills in how many were sown. A lot can't give more than it has.
  • Harvested from shows a lot's descent, newest first: the plant it came from, that plant's sowing, the lot that sowing came from, and so on back to the first lot. Click a lot in it to go to that lot.
  • Labels prints a label for each lot with seed left, for a tube or a packet, with a QR code that opens the lot.
  • New seed lot is for seed you already have: from a stock centre, a colleague or a drawer.

The selection plate

Grow a T2 lot on its selection (kanamycin, hygromycin, Basta) and type how many seedlings were Resistant and how many Sensitive. The Selection column says what the counts mean, by a χ² test at p = 0.05: fits 3:1, one insertion; fits 15:1, two insertions; all resistant: homozygous (from 30 seedlings up); or that they fit neither, or that more need counting to tell. Hover over it for the χ² values.

The schedule

The Schedule counts days after each sowing: move it out of the cold after 3 days, count germination at 7, transplant at 14, harvest at 70 (Arabidopsis under long days). A field crop transplants from the nursery at 30 days, flowers at 90 and is harvested at 120. Stored seed comes up for a germination test every two years. Change any of them under Configure → Schedule rules for your species; Home shows what is due.

In your own words

Everything here is a setting, under Configure: what a plant, a tray, a growth room and a sowing are called; the words for the two dates (Start date is called, End date is called); the Stages a sowing goes through; the Kinds of place (growth room, greenhouse, nursery, field); the units seed is counted in; and Young until and Old after, the days that colour the dot before a plant's ID blue (a seedling), then green, then red. Add columns of your own, to plants, trays, lines, sowings, crosses or seed lots, as in Any other organism.

Troubleshooting

The Seeds tab isn't there

Its database was made before seed lots, or without them. Under Configure, tick Seed stocks and save.

A harvest gave the wrong generation

The generation follows the plant's sowing; if that sowing has none, the lot has none either. Type it in the lot, and give the sowing its generation so the next harvest follows on.

Sow is greyed out on a lot

The lot is someone else's: taking seed from it changes it, so it follows the same rule as any record. Ask them or an admin, or make a sowing and pick the lot under Sown from lot without taking seed.

"Only 5 mg left" when sowing

The lot has less seed than Seed taken. Check the amount, or correct the lot's Left if seed was added to it.
