Data and help

AI assistants

Tell an AI assistant what you did; it proposes the records, and you approve them in BioManager.

Lab server or desktop

BioManager has no AI inside it. If you already use an assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Cherry Studio or another app that speaks MCP), you can connect it to your lab. Tell it what you did, in your own words or with a photo of your notebook (litter in 102 yesterday, 7 pups; moved the two old breeders from 88 to 91; injected day 3 of the TMX cohort this morning), and it turns that into records: as one proposal, which you approve in BioManager. It never changes anything itself.

Connect an assistant

Open Help → Connect an AI assistant (it is also under Settings → AI assistant & tokens, and on an empty Proposed changes). It shows the ways in for your BioManager, with its address filled in. Nothing needs installing, except for Claude Desktop on the lab network (below).

From claude.ai, the Claude apps or ChatGPT

These apps reach BioManager from the internet, so this works when your lab's server is on the internet: its admin turns on internet access (in the server's runbook). Then the Connect page shows the connector address, ending in /api/v1/mcp. The desktop app can't be reached this way; use the way below.

  1. Add BioManager as a custom connector with that address. In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector (on a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner adds it first). In ChatGPT: Settings → Connectors, with developer mode on, then create a connector with OAuth.
  2. When it asks you to connect, a BioManager page opens asking for a connection code. On the Connect page (on the lab network), press Get a connection code, type it in, and press Allow. A code works once, for 10 minutes.
  3. That's it, on the web and in the phone app alike. The connection is listed under Settings → AI assistant & tokens with the app's name; revoke it there to end it.

From Claude Code, Cursor, Cherry Studio or Claude Desktop

These run on a computer that can reach BioManager: the same computer as the desktop app, or any computer on the lab network for a lab server. No internet access is needed. You don't need to know how the connection works:

  1. Let your assistant set itself up. On the Connect page, press Copy the message and send it in a chat with your coding assistant. The message has your BioManager's address in it; the assistant adds BioManager by itself, then tells you how to sign in.
  2. Sign in. In Claude Code, type /mcp and choose biomanager. BioManager opens in your browser, asking whether to let the app connect: press Allow. There is no token to copy or keep.
  • To do it yourself in Claude Code, the Connect page also has the one command to run in a terminal.
  • For an app that can't sign in, and for Claude Desktop, press Make a token: it makes a token that may Read and propose, and the next page shows the setup to copy for each app. Claude Desktop runs a small program from BioManager's source, mcp/biomanager_mcp.py; the steps are in mcp/README.md.

Use it

Ask it: Log today's work from my notes: … It looks up each animal, cage and experiment you mention, asks you when something could be more than one record, and sends one proposal with a link to it.

  • The assistant reads what you can read, as you. Revoke its token under Settings → AI assistant & tokens to cut it off.

What it can propose

  • In the mouse colony, zebrafish, fly and worm databases and any other organism's: the everyday records. New animals, cages, tanks and vials; litters, weaning, moves and deaths; crosses, clutches and flips; weights and readings; scheduled jobs done.
  • In an experiment: a step done, a readout, animals added, its status, a note.
  • In the notebook: a line in the Log of today's daily log, with the time. If the page is open, the line appears in it within a few seconds.
  • It can't delete anything, sign a page, or change who may see what.
  • It can also tell you what is due, as Home lists it, and plan your day from it.

Approving

A proposal tells you (the bell) and waits under Proposed changes in your account menu.

  • Each proposal is one summary in plain words: what will change in each database, with any warnings at the top (a litter date that would be replaced, pups too young to wean). Show each change lists every record, before → after.
  • Approve makes every change at once; Discard makes none. There is no approving one change at a time: if something is wrong, discard it and ask the assistant for a corrected proposal.
  • An approved proposal is one batch in Batch history, marked with the assistant's name, so Undo takes it back whole (a line added to the notebook stays).
  • If a record it touches was changed after the proposal was made, or a change no longer goes through, nothing is applied and the page says which record and why.
  • Only you can approve your proposals, and only signed in to BioManager: no token can. A proposal you leave waits two weeks, then expires.

Troubleshooting

The assistant says it can't reach BioManager

For Claude Code, Cursor or Cherry Studio: check that BioManager is running, that the address in the setup is its address (the desktop app's is on Settings → AI assistant & tokens), and that the token hasn't been revoked or expired. For claude.ai, the Claude apps or ChatGPT: check that the connector address opens from a phone off the lab's Wi-Fi (it should show a short message asking for an API token), and that it ends in /api/v1/mcp.

Connecting says the connection code isn't right

A code works once and for 10 minutes. Press Get a connection code again and use the new one.

Approving says a record changed after the proposal was made

Someone (or you) changed it meanwhile, so the proposal may be out of date. Discard it and ask the assistant to propose again.
