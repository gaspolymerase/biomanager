Your databases

Plasmids

Plasmid records with their sequence and map, and the box each tube is in.

  • Show them as a Table, by storage Boxes, or as a List; chips for Mine, Has sequence, In a box.
  • Sequence, beside each plasmid's number in the table, opens its sequence and map (Add sequence when it has none yet); the number opens the same page.
  • New plasmid takes a name, backbone, insert, resistance, owner and a box position (blank takes the next free one) — or paste a list of names to make several. Drop in a SnapGene .dna, GenBank, FASTA or plain-text sequence.
  • Open a plasmid to see its sequence and features on an interactive map you can edit (edits save themselves), full screen if you like; Storage and Properties are the other tabs. Storage also lists the viruses made from the plasmid (Viruses). Its address carries its number: plasmid #12 is …/plasmid/12.
  • Each tube keeps its miniprep's Concentration (ng/µL) and 260/280, in the sheet and on its Storage tab, which also lists the notebook pages that link it.
  • Belongs to: Personal, or Lab common — anyone can edit it (a shared stock plasmid), while only its owner or an admin changes whose it is or deletes it. The Lab common chip lists them.
  • Select plasmids to set owner, resistance or Belongs to, move them to a box, or delete. Moved into another box with no position, a plasmid takes its next free cell. Import from Excel brings in your old list.
