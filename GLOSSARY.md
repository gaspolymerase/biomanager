# BioManager's words

The words a lab uses in BioManager, as the app means them. Where one word
could mean two things, this file picks one meaning, and the code, the pages
and the guide follow it.

## The mouse colony

**Cage**:
A mouse cage, with an owner, a place on a rack and a purpose.
_Avoid_: box, housing (in the colony; "housing" is the any-organism word)

**Purpose**:
What a cage or tank is for, one word from the lab's own list.
_Avoid_: type, status

**Breeding** (cage):
A mating cage, mating now. The only cage with **Litter born**, **Genotyping** and **Wean**; its litters are its pups.
_Avoid_: mating cage (as a purpose), breeder cage

**Breeder** (cage):
The lab's breeding stock: mice kept to be bred. Each person keeps part of it, so a Breeder cage starts shared with the lab, and anyone may **Pick** from it.
_Avoid_: stock, breeding stock cage, Breeding

**Experiment** (cage):
A cage of mice in an experiment.
_Avoid_: Exp, Experiments

**Active**:
A cage that holds living mice, and nothing more. An empty cage is simply not active.
_Avoid_: in use, retired (as the opposite)

**Retire**:
To take an empty cage out of its rack and clear its purpose. An action, not a purpose.
_Avoid_: Retired (as a purpose)

**Pick**:
To take a mouse from a Breeder cage into your own name, to set up a mating.
_Avoid_: claim, take

**Litter**:
The pups born together in a Breeding cage.
_Avoid_: cohort (in the colony; "cohort" is the any-organism word)

## Zebrafish

**Tank**:
A zebrafish tank, holding a group of fish with a headcount or named individuals.
_Avoid_: cage

**Breeder** (tank):
The lab's breeding stock of fish, shared with the lab as a mouse Breeder cage is.
_Avoid_: breeding tank (it meant this before; for mice "Breeding" means mating)

**Mating** (tank):
A tank being crossed now, set up from a male and a female tank, until it is marked **Returned** and its fish go back.
_Avoid_: breeding tank, cross tank

**Stock** (tank):
A tank keeping a line's fish; a new tank's purpose unless you choose another. A purpose for tanks only: a mouse cage has no Stock purpose.
_Avoid_: holding

**Clutch**:
The eggs from one fertilisation, recorded against its cross.
_Avoid_: litter, batch

## Who may change what

**Owner**:
The lab member a record belongs to, who may edit it, share it or give it away.
_Avoid_: creator, user

**Shared**:
A record the whole lab (or one project group) may edit, while it stays its owner's to give away.
_Avoid_: public, common (for cages and tanks)

**Lab common**:
A plasmid or inventory item the whole lab may edit: Shared, in those databases' words.
_Avoid_: public

**Project group**:
Some of the lab working on one project, who can be given what is shared with them.
_Avoid_: team, subgroup
