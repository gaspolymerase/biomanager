## BioManager 1.5.8

### New

- **In the Mac app, buttons beside the window's close, minimise and zoom buttons**, as in Safari: hide or show the sidebar (⌘B), search everything (⌘K), and go back and forward (⌘[ and ⌘]). Back and forward grey out where there is nowhere to go. Search takes the place of the search box at the top right.
  - Mac 应用里，窗口按钮旁边多了几个按钮：隐藏或显示侧边栏（⌘B）、全局搜索（⌘K）、后退和前进（⌘[、⌘]）。右上角的搜索框由这个搜索按钮代替。

### Works differently

- **A collapsed sidebar names its places.** Each is a tile with its icon over its name in small type, the groups are parted by a thin line, and **Settings** and **Help** stay at the bottom while the rest scrolls; before, the icons sat close together with no names, and Settings and Help could fall off the bottom of a short window.
  - 收起的侧边栏现在每个图标下面都写着名字，分组之间有细线；**设置** 和 **帮助** 固定在底部，不会再被挤出窗口。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.5.8, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
