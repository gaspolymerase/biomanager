## BioManager 1.16.0

### New

- **The trace around each difference.** **Verify sequencing** draws, beside each difference an `.ab1` read shows, the stretch of chromatogram around it, the four dyes in their usual colours and the base in question shaded, so a mismatch can be told from a poor peak without opening another program.
  - **每个差异附近的峰图。** **验证测序** 会在 `.ab1` 读段的每个差异旁边画出它附近的一段峰图，四种染料用常见的颜色，所说的碱基加底色，不用打开别的软件就能分辨是真的突变还是峰不好。
- **A read that looks like a mix of two clones** (a second peak under many of its good bases) is said so, with its trace: pick a single colony and sequence it again. When the second peaks start at one place and carry on, it says where: two clones that differ by an insertion or deletion there.
  - **看起来像两个克隆混合的读段**（很多高质量碱基下面有第二个峰）会被指出，并附上峰图：挑单克隆重新测序。第二个峰从某处开始一直延续时，它会说出位置：两个克隆在那里相差一个插入或缺失。

### Works differently

- **An AI assistant's breeding plan** counts every litter a mating has time for before the date you need the mice (or as many as you say), counts the pairs already in Breeding cages as matings under way and leaves their mice out of the free breeders, plans up to five genes, and says when two crosses it lists would need the same breeders or a breeder is in an experiment.
  - **AI 助手的繁殖计划** 会算上每对在你需要小鼠的日期之前能生的每一窝（或你说的窝数），把繁殖笼里已经在配的对算作进行中的配种，不再把它们的小鼠算作空闲的繁殖鼠，最多可以算五个基因；列出的两种配对要用同一批繁殖鼠，或有繁殖鼠在实验中时，也会说明。
- **The animal-use report** counts your own organisms (under their own protocol, else their housing's, else their line's); a mouse with no protocol of its own is counted under its mother's, else its father's; and a litter's pups that have no record of their own are counted as born under their parents' protocol and strain, not as one line for the whole lab.
  - **动物使用报告** 会统计你们自己的物种（取它自己的方案，再取饲养单元的，再取品系的）；没有方案的小鼠算在母亲的方案下，再看父亲的；窝里有数但没有单独记录的仔鼠按出生计入父母的方案和品系，不再是全实验室一行。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.16.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database's tables. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
