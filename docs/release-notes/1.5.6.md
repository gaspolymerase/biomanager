## BioManager 1.5.6

### New

- **Import from Excel reads Chinese sheets.** Headers in Chinese are matched as their English names are (性别, 出生日期, 笼号, 基因型, 质粒名称, 载体, 货号…), in every database; 公/母 and 雄/雌 are sexes, and 种鼠, 实验, 鉴定, 处死 are mouse statuses. A CSV saved by Excel on a Chinese Windows is read as Chinese instead of garbled letters.
  - **从 Excel 导入** 现在能读中文表格：中文表头（性别、出生日期、笼号、基因型、质粒名称、载体、货号……）会自动匹配；公/母、雄/雌会识别为性别，种鼠、实验、鉴定、处死会识别为小鼠状态；中文版 Windows 的 Excel 另存的 CSV 也不会再变成乱码。

### Works differently

- **A cage written once, with blank cells below.** A sheet filled in by eye often writes a cage (or a rack, box or tank) on its first row only; the rows below used to come in with none. Such a column now offers **Its blank cells take the value above them** on the match page, ticked, and the preview says how many cells it filled. Untick it if a blank in your sheet means none.
  - 只在第一行写了笼号、下面留空的表格：笼、架子、盒子或鱼缸这样的列会出现已勾选的 **空白单元格沿用上方的值**，预览会说明填了多少格；如果空白表示“没有”，取消勾选即可。
- **On a Mac, the window's close, minimise and zoom buttons sit square in its corner** ([#63](https://github.com/gaspolymerase/biomanager/pull/63)), and on the welcome pages BioManager's name sits on their line, with the picture and its words centred.
  - Mac 窗口左上角的三个按钮与边缘对齐；欢迎页上 BioManager 的名字和这三个按钮在同一行，图片和文字居中。

### Fixed

- **A plasmid's concentration written with its unit** (`152.6 ng/µl`) is read as the concentration; it went into the notes. Another unit, such as µg/µl, is still kept in the notes rather than guessed at.
  - 写了单位的质粒浓度（如 `152.6 ng/µl`）现在会读成浓度，而不是放进备注。

These came from a benchmark that imports 740 messy sheets with known contents (`benchmarks/` in the repository): with every suggestion accepted, 99.8% of mouse values and 99.9% of plasmid values now come in exactly right, and none is changed or left out without a word.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.5.6, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database.
