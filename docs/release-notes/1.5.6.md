## BioManager 1.5.6

### New

- **Import from Excel reads more of the sheets labs really keep.**
  - **A cage, rack, box or tank written once per group**, with blank cells below it, no longer leaves the rows below without one. The match page offers *Its blank cells take the value above them* for such a column, already ticked when the column looks filled in that way, and the preview says how many cells it filled. Untick it where a blank means none.
  - **Chinese column headers are recognised** (性别, 出生日期, 笼号, 质粒名称, 货号…), as are 公/母/雄/雌 for sex and the Chinese mouse statuses.
  - **A CSV saved by Excel on a Chinese Windows** is read as Chinese, not garbled.
  - **A plasmid's concentration written with its unit**, such as “152.6 ng/µl”, goes into the concentration instead of the notes.
  - 从 Excel 导入能读懂更多实验室真实使用的表格：每组只写一次的笼号、架号、盒号（下面留空）可以沿用上方的值；能识别中文列标题和中文的性别、状态；中文 Windows 下 Excel 保存的 CSV 不再乱码；带单位的质粒浓度（如“152.6 ng/µl”）会填进浓度一栏。

### Changed

- **The Mac window's close, minimise and zoom buttons sit in the corner** as other Mac apps place them: as far in from the left as from the top. BioManager's name on the welcome page sits on their line, clear of them, and the welcome page's picture and words are one column in the middle.
  - Mac 窗口左上角的红黄绿按钮位置与其他 Mac 应用一致；欢迎页上的 BioManager 名称与按钮在同一行并留出间距，图片和文字居中排列。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.5.6, or download it below. A Mac app on 1.5.2 or earlier may not offer it (fixed in 1.5.3): download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes from 1.5.5. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
