## BioManager 2.6.0

### New

- **Change a plasmid on its Sequence tab.** Pick bases and **Edit…**: type over them, insert bases after them, or delete them, or add a feature on them with its name, type and direction. With bases picked you can also just type a base letter to start, or press **Delete** to remove them. Click a feature to rename it, change its type, turn it round or remove it. Each edit is saved at once — the features after it move with it, one it runs through grows or shrinks — and **Undo** (⌘Z) takes it back. Under the sequence: **Make it linear** or circular, **Full screen**, the GenBank and FASTA downloads, and the feature library's buttons.
  - **在序列标签页修改质粒。** 选中碱基后点 **编辑…**：改写它们、在后面插入碱基、删除它们，或在它们上面加一个特征（名称、类型和方向）。选中碱基后直接敲一个碱基字母也能开始，按 **Delete** 则删除它们。点一个特征可以改名、改类型、调转方向或删除。每次编辑都立即保存——后面的特征跟着移动，被改动穿过的特征会变长或变短——**撤销**（⌘Z）可以撤回。序列下方有 **改为线性** 或环状、**全屏**、GenBank 和 FASTA 下载，以及元件库的按钮。
- **Site-directed mutagenesis.** Pick bases (one is enough) and **Mutagenesis…**: replace them, insert after them, delete them, or write the change to the protein (*K80A*). BioManager designs the two back-to-back primers, as NEB's Q5 kit has them, with their Tm and any hairpin or self-dimer; says what the change does to each protein; and lists the silent changes nearby that add or remove an enzyme's site, for a diagnostic digest or to free a site. **Make it** adds the new plasmid, **Made from** this one, with both primers in **Primers** to order.
  - **定点突变。** 选中碱基（一个就够）后点 **定点突变…**：替换、在后面插入、删除，或直接写出蛋白的改变（如 *K80A*）。BioManager 会按 NEB Q5 试剂盒的方式设计两条背靠背的引物，给出它们的 Tm、发夹和自身二聚体；说明这个改动对每个蛋白的影响；并列出附近不改变蛋白、却能加入或去掉酶切位点的同义突变，方便做鉴定酶切或腾出位点。**创建** 会新建这个质粒，**来源质粒** 记为本质粒，两条引物也放进 **引物** 库待订购。
- **In-Fusion and Gateway in Assemble a plasmid.** **In-Fusion** is the overlap assembly with the shorter 15-base ends Takara's kit uses, the vector cut rather than amplified. **Gateway** has its own panel: choose the plasmid carrying the gene (between **attB** sites for BP, **attL** for LR) and its partner (a pDONR with **attP**, or a destination vector with **attR**), and BP or LR makes the entry or expression clone — the gene onto the partner's backbone, the att sites converted, the ccdB cassette removed, and both plasmids kept under **Made from**.
  - **组装质粒里的 In-Fusion 和 Gateway。** **In-Fusion** 是用 Takara 试剂盒那种较短的 15 碱基末端做的重叠组装，载体是切开而不是扩增。**Gateway** 有自己的面板：选带有基因的质粒（BP 用 **attB** 位点，LR 用 **attL**）和它的搭档（带 **attP** 的 pDONR，或带 **attR** 的目的载体），BP 或 LR 就会做出入门克隆或表达克隆——基因接到搭档的骨架上，att 位点随之转换，ccdB 盒被去掉，两个质粒都记进 **来源质粒**。
- **Compare two plasmids.** **Compare with…** on a plasmid's **Overview** lines it up against another in the lab and lists every base that differs, the feature it falls in and what it does to a protein, with the two maps side by side and the differences marked on each. It matches however the two origins sit, or whichever strand the other was saved on — so it answers which of two near-identical constructs is in the tube, and what a colleague's "same plasmid" actually changed.
  - **比较两个质粒。** 质粒 **概览** 上的 **与…比较** 会把它和实验室里另一个对齐，列出每一个不同的碱基、它落在哪个特征里、对蛋白有什么影响，两张图谱并排显示，不同之处在各自图上都标出来。不管两者的起点在哪、另一个存在哪条链上都能对上——所以它能回答管子里到底是两个几乎一样的构建体中的哪一个，以及同事口中「同一个质粒」究竟改了什么。
- **A sequence by its number.** **New plasmid** and **Replace the sequence** now take an Addgene number (*Addgene #11150*, or just *11150*) or a GenBank accession (*L09137*) under **From Addgene or NCBI**: the record comes in with its features, and the name and where it came from fill in if they are empty. A server with no internet says so and you download the file yourself.
  - **按编号获取序列。** **新建质粒** 和 **替换序列** 里现在可以在 **从 Addgene 或 NCBI 获取** 下填 Addgene 编号（*Addgene #11150*，或直接写 *11150*）或 GenBank 登录号（*L09137*）：记录会连同特征一起导入，名称和来源为空时也会自动填上。服务器没有联网时会提示你，自己下载文件即可。

### Works differently

- **BioManager draws and edits plasmids on its own now.** The sequence editor it used to borrow is gone, and with it about 9 MB that every plasmid page had to load, so the page opens faster. Everything it did is on the Sequence tab, where the rest of the plasmid already was. A virus's or a glycerol stock's plasmid shows the same map and sequence, to read.
  - **质粒的绘制和编辑现在都由 BioManager 自己完成。** 以前借用的序列编辑器已经去掉，每个质粒页面少载入约 9 MB，打开更快。它原来能做的事都在序列标签页上，质粒的其他内容本来就在那里。病毒或甘油菌对应的质粒也显示同样的图谱和序列，只读。
- **The search palette (⌘K) is a sheet of glass** over the page, with a larger field and roomier rows, and **Ask the guide** as a card of its own.
  - **搜索面板（⌘K）变成了一块玻璃**，浮在页面之上，输入框更大、每行更宽松，**向用户指南提问** 单独成为一张卡片。
- **Settings says each thing once, where it belongs.** How you sign in (your password, and other accounts) is part of **Profile**. In **Notifications**, a mouse picked from your breeder cage follows the **Transfers** switch, and the reminder email is one **Daily email** row. **Text size** shows only in the Mac app, the one place that can set it. The welcome tour moved to **Help → Welcome tour**, and **Batch history** is reached from **History**.
  - **设置里每件事只出现一次，并放在该在的地方。** 登录方式（密码和其他账号）并入 **个人资料**。**通知** 里，有人从你的种鼠笼取走小鼠归入 **转移** 开关，提醒邮件简化为一行 **每日邮件**。**文字大小** 只在 Mac 应用里显示，因为只有它能设置。欢迎导览移到 **帮助 → 欢迎导览**，**批量操作记录** 从 **历史** 进入。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.6.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes in this version. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).

  A server that must never reach out can set `BIOMANAGER_SEQUENCE_FETCH=0` in `.env` to hide **From Addgene or NCBI**.
