## BioManager 1.3.0

### New

- **Pages in pages, as in Notion.**
  - **/page** makes a new page inside the one you are writing and opens it in a BioManager tab.
  - **Link to page**, or typing `[[`, finds a page that exists, or makes a new one named as you typed.
  - **Turn into page** makes a line a page of its own, with the line's words as its title.
  - A link to a page shows that page's title as it is now, so renaming a page renames every link to it. Alone on a line, it shows as a page row.
  - In the sidebar, a page's pages sit under it; the arrow beside it shows or hides them.
- **Linked from**: under a page's title, the pages that link to it, once there are any.

### Works differently

- **Topics are folders.** A new page goes in the topic it was made from, else the topic you have open, else *Inbox*, whatever kind of page it is. Changing a page's kind never moves it. Pages you already have stay where they are.
  - This is new for today's daily log, meeting notes, protocols, experiments started from a protocol and the colony's **Add to notebook**, which used to go to topics of their own.
- **The top of a page reads like a file path**: its topic, then the pages it sits in, then the page.
  - Click the topic to move the page: search your topics, or type a name to make a new one. The pages inside it move with it.
  - The *Topic* box under the title is gone.
- **The icon beside the title is the page's kind.** Click it to change the kind; each choice says what it adds (an experiment's times and status, a protocol's versions, a meeting's series). The kind box above the title is gone.
- The date, status and tags under the title are chips.
- **Links open in BioManager.** A link to one of the app's own pages opens in a BioManager tab instead of a browser tab; links to other websites open in your browser.

### Fixed

- A change typed just before following a link out of a notebook page could be lost. The page is now saved before it is left.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.3.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and a copy is taken before the database is upgraded.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  The database upgrades itself on start, adding the page each notebook page was made in; every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
