## BioManager 2.11.0

### New

- **Cite a paper in any notebook page.** Type `[@` (or `/cite`), search your paper notes in Reading by title, author, year, journal or DOI, and the citation goes in as it reads in a paper: (Smith, 2024), (Smith & Lee, 2024) or (Smith et al., 2024), with a Chinese first author written whole, (王小明 等, 2024). Hover it for the full reference; click it to open the paper's note. The words come from the note's authors and year, so correcting the note corrects every citation of it, and the note's **Linked from** lists the pages that cite it. Add **References** (`/references`) at the end of a page to list every paper it cites, in the order they first appear. Papers imported from Zotero are ready to cite.
  - **在任何记录本页面里引用文献。** 输入 `[@`（或 `/cite`），按标题、作者、年份、期刊或 DOI 搜索 **文献** 里的论文笔记，引用会按论文里的写法插入：(Smith, 2024)、(Smith & Lee, 2024) 或 (Smith et al., 2024)，中文第一作者写全名，如 (王小明 等, 2024)。鼠标停在上面显示完整的参考文献，点击打开那篇文献的笔记。引用的文字来自笔记里的作者和年份，所以改了笔记，所有引用它的地方都会跟着改；笔记标题下方的 **被 N 个页面链接** 会列出引用它的页面。在页面末尾加 **参考文献**（`/references`），按第一次出现的顺序列出页面引用的每篇文献。从 Zotero 导入的文献可以直接引用。
- **Choose how BioManager looks** in **Settings → Appearance & language**. Pick a theme, **Glass**, **Paper white**, **Night** or **Bench**, and it sets the app icon, colour, mode, font and rows at once; change any of them afterwards to make the look your own. The app icon comes in eight drawings (a DNA helix, mouse, zebrafish, worm, fly, cryobox, microtube and Petri dish) and eight colours, on a coloured, white or black background, and shows in the browser tab, the sidebar and the Mac app's Dock. **Mode** keeps BioManager light or dark whatever the computer uses, and **Rows of a sheet** sets the row height and whether divider lines, stripes or both run between rows. Each person's choices are their own.
  - **在 设置 → 外观与语言 里选择 BioManager 的样子。** 选一个主题：**玻璃**、**纸面**、**夜间** 或 **实验台**，应用图标、颜色、模式、字体和表格的行会一起换好；之后再改其中任何一项，就成了你自己的搭配。应用图标有八个图案（DNA 螺旋、小鼠、斑马鱼、线虫、果蝇、冻存盒、离心管和培养皿）和八种颜色，底色可选彩色、白底或黑底，显示在浏览器标签、侧边栏和 Mac 应用的 Dock 上。**模式** 可以让 BioManager 始终浅色或始终深色，不跟随电脑；**表格的行** 设置行高，以及行之间用分隔线、斑马纹还是两者都用。每个人的选择只影响自己。

### Works differently

- **BioManager has a new icon**: a DNA helix in jade green, in the apps, the browser tab and on the website. If you had chosen an icon colour before, it becomes the nearest of the eight new ones.
  - **BioManager 换了新图标**：青玉色的 DNA 螺旋，用在各个应用、浏览器标签和网站上。如果你以前选过图标颜色，会换成八种新颜色里最接近的一种。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.11.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
