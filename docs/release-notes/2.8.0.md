## BioManager 2.8.0

### New

- **What the orders cost.** The orders page has a **Spending** view beside **Table** and **Board**: what the lab asked for, month by month, in a chart you can point at, by **Person**, **Project group**, **Vendor** or **Category**, over the last 12 months, this year, the last 3 months, all of it or the months you choose, with a table below and **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet. An order costs its Price times its Quantity (or its Price alone, if your lab writes the whole order's price there); a cancelled one counts nowhere.
  - **订单花了多少。** 订单页在 **表格** 和 **看板** 旁边新增了 **花费** 视图：实验室申请的订单按月汇总成可以指向查看的图表，可以按 **成员**、**项目组**、**供应商** 或 **类别** 分组，时间可以是最近 12 个月、本年度、最近 3 个月、全部或自选月份；下方有明细表，**下载 CSV** 可以用电子表格打开。订单金额为价格乘以数量（如果你们实验室在价格里写的是整个订单的价格，则只算价格）；已取消的订单不计入。
- **Spending limits.** An admin sets a limit per year (from the month your year starts) or per month, for the lab, a person or a project group, in **Limits and settings**. The Spending view shows how much of each is used, and saving an order that takes someone past a limit says so.
  - **花费限额。** 管理员可以在 **限额和设置** 里为全实验室、个人或项目组设置每年（从你们年度开始的月份算起）或每月的限额。花费视图显示每项已用多少；保存订单时如果让某人超出限额，会给出提示。
- **One list of vendors.** **Vendors**, at the top of the orders page and of every list with a vendor, lists every vendor your records name, with its orders, what they cost in the last 12 months and when it was last ordered from. Keep its website, contact, email, phone and your customer number there. An admin or a facility manager can rename a vendor or **Merge** several spellings (*Sigma*, *Sigma-Aldrich*) on every record at once; Batch history undoes it.
  - **供应商集中管理。** 订单页和所有带供应商的列表顶部都有 **供应商**，列出记录里写到的每个供应商、它的订单、最近 12 个月的金额和最近一次订购时间，还可以记下网站、联系人、邮箱、电话和你们的客户编号。管理员或设施管理员可以给供应商改名，或把几种写法（*Sigma*、*Sigma-Aldrich*）**合并**，所有记录一起改；可以在批量操作记录里撤销。
- **A plasmid's backbone and insert link to the lab's plasmids.** The **Backbone** and **Insert** columns offer the lab's plasmids as you type, and the usual vectors (pcDNA3.1, pLKO.1, pAAV…). Pick one, or type `@` and its name, and the plasmid is listed under **Made from**; the one named lists it under **Used to make**, so a gene's plasmid shows every construct made with it. **Link them**, for an admin, links Backbones and Inserts written before.
  - **质粒的骨架和插入片段可以关联到实验室的质粒。** **骨架** 和 **插入片段** 两列在输入时会列出实验室的质粒和常用载体（pcDNA3.1、pLKO.1、pAAV…）。选一个，或输入 `@` 加质粒名称，这个质粒就会列进 **来源质粒**，被写到的质粒在 **用于制备** 里列出它；这样一个基因的质粒会列出用它构建的所有载体。管理员可以用 **建立关联** 把以前写下的骨架和插入片段一次关联好。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.8.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  The database gains one table, for the vendors' details, by itself when the new version starts; nothing already stored changes. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
