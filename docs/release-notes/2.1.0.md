## BioManager 2.1.0

### New

- **Plant databases.** **Add database** offers **Plants** (pots and trays in growth rooms, growth chambers or a greenhouse) and **Field crops** (plots in a field, raised first in a nursery and transplanted). A plant is **Sown** and **Harvested**, not born and dead, and has no sex; a sowing has its stage (seedling, transplanted, flowering…, or *in the nursery*), and the **Schedule** counts days after sowing: out of the cold, count germination, transplant, harvest. Places are growth rooms, greenhouses, nurseries and fields; a field is a grid of plots.
  - **植物数据库。** **添加数据库** 新增 **植物** （生长室、人工气候箱或温室里的盆钵和托盘）和 **大田作物** （试验田中的小区，先在苗圃育苗再移栽）。植株记录的是 **播种** 和 **收获**，而不是出生和死亡，也没有性别；播种批次有自己的阶段（幼苗、已移栽、开花……，大田作物还有 *在苗圃*），**日程** 按播种后的天数提醒：结束低温层积、统计发芽、移栽、收种。场所可以是生长室、温室、苗圃和试验田；试验田是小区组成的网格。
- **Seed lots.** A plant database's **Seeds** tab keeps every seed lot: its line and generation, where it was harvested from, how much is left, where it is kept and its germination. The wheat button on a plant, a sowing or a cross **harvests** its seed into a new lot a generation on (T1 plants give T2 seed; seed from a cross is F1) and can mark the plants harvested; **Sow** on a lot starts a sowing from it and takes the seed off what is left. Each lot shows its descent back to the first lot, and **Labels** prints them for tubes or packets.
  - **种子批次。** 植物数据库的 **种子** 标签页记录每个种子批次：品系和代数、收获来源、剩余量、存放位置和发芽率。在植株、播种批次或杂交上点麦穗按钮即可 **收种**，生成晚一代的新批次（T1 植株收获 T2 种子；杂交收获的种子为 F1），并可把植株标记为已收获；在批次上点 **播种** 会用它新建一次播种，并从剩余量中扣除取用的种子。每个批次都显示它一直追溯到最初批次的来历，**标签** 可为管子或纸袋打印标签。
- **The selection plate.** Type a T2 lot's resistant and sensitive seedlings and the sheet says whether they fit 3:1 (one insertion), 15:1 (two) or are all resistant (homozygous), by χ².
  - **筛选平板。** 填写 T2 批次的抗性苗和敏感苗数量，表格会按 χ² 检验说明它们是否符合 3:1（单拷贝插入）、15:1（两个位点），或全部抗性（纯合）。
- **A line's construct.** A column of your own can be a **Plasmid** from the Plasmids database, linked to it: a plant line names the construct it was transformed with, and whether it is transgenic and under which biosafety permit.
  - **品系的构建体。** 自己加的列可以是质粒数据库中的 **质粒**，并链接过去：植物品系可以记下转化所用的构建体、是否转基因以及对应的生物安全许可。

### Works differently

- **Any organism database speaks in its own words.** Under **Configure**: what its two dates are called (**Start date is called**: Hatched, Sown; **End date is called**), the **Stages** a cohort goes through and the **Kinds of place** its racks and rooms can be. Leave **Sexes** empty and the Sex column goes. **Young until** and **Old after** colour the dot before an ID by age, as the mouse colony's does.
  - **任何物种数据库都可以用自己的说法。** 在 **配置** 里设置两个日期的名称（**起始日期名称**：孵化、播种；**结束日期名称**）、一批个体经历的 **阶段**，以及笼架和房间可以是哪些 **场所类型**。把 **性别** 留空，性别列就会隐藏。**幼龄截至** 和 **老龄始于** 会像小鼠库那样按年龄给编号前的圆点上色。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.1.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version adds a table (seed lots) and two columns to another (a sowing's seed lot and generation); updating adds them, and keeps everything else as it was. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
