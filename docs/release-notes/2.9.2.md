## BioManager 2.9.2

### New

- **Add another after this** in a new mouse's form. Tick it beside **Create mouse**, and each save keeps the form open for the next mouse: its strain, cage, sex and the rest stay filled in, the tag and note are cleared, and the form shows the next free ID. The sheet shows the new mice when you close the form; the box stays ticked until you untick it.
  - 新建小鼠的表单里多了 **保存后再加一只**。勾上 **创建小鼠** 旁边的这个选项，每次保存后表单不关：品系、笼子、性别等都留着，自定义标识和备注清空，并显示下一个空闲编号。关上表单后表格里就有新加的小鼠；这个勾一直保留，直到你取消。
- **Numbers line up on the right** in every sheet, heading and all: a mouse's age, a cage's mice, a reagent's quantity, **Low at** and **Pack size**, a stock's vials, a plasmid's length, and any number field of your own, so the digits sit under each other.
  - **每张表里的数字都靠右对齐**，列标题也一样：小鼠的年龄、笼子里的只数、试剂的数量、**低库存线** 和 **包装规格**、库存的管数、质粒的长度，以及你自己加的数字字段，数字上下对齐。

### Fixed

- **Set field**: clicking one of a column's choices puts it in **value** and leaves the panel open for **Set field**. In 2.9.1 the click closed the panel, so the choice seemed to do nothing.
  - **设置字段**：点这一列的某个选项，会把它填进 **值**，面板保持打开，可以接着点 **设置字段**。2.9.1 里点选项会把面板关掉，看起来像没反应。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.9.2, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
