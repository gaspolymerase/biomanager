## BioManager 2.7.0

### New

- **Design an experiment with an AI assistant, and set it up in one go.** Ask Claude or another assistant connected to BioManager to *Design an experiment* (*does drug X reduce airway inflammation in our HDM model? Start in about six weeks*). It first asks what your records don't say — the controls, the readout day, who does what — then gives you the plan: the groups and controls, the main readout, how many animals and which, the regimen day by day from your saved regimens, and the test, with a published source for any dose the lab hasn't used. When you say the plan is right, it sends it all as one proposal: the experiment (planned, so nothing is due until you press **Start**), a **Breeding** cage for each mating still needed, the to-dos, each due when it has to be done, and the calendar entries. The BioManager plugin is 1.7.0.
  - **让 AI 助手设计实验，并一次安排好。** 让 Claude 或其他连接了 BioManager 的助手 *设计实验*（*X 药能否减轻我们 HDM 模型的气道炎症？大约六周后开始*）。它会先问清记录里找不到的事——对照、观测日、谁做什么——再给你计划：分组和对照、主要观测指标、需要多少只动物以及用哪些、来自你保存的处理方案的逐日给药安排、统计检验；实验室没用过的剂量会附上已发表的来源。你确认计划无误后，它把这一切作为一个提议发出：实验（计划中，按 **开始** 之前没有任何待办）、每个还需要的配种一个 **配种笼**、待办（每条在需要完成的那天到期）和日历条目。BioManager 插件为 1.7.0。

### Works differently

- **A big colony opens faster.** The **Breeders** tab of a lab with hundreds of Breeder cages shows the first 100, with **Show 100 more** under them; the search and **Alive only** still look through every cage. In a lab of 100,000 mice it opens in under a second instead of 1.6 s. **Home** comes up about a quarter faster there, and the app starts faster too.
  - **大型小鼠库打开更快。** 种鼠笼有几百个的实验室，**种鼠** 标签页先显示前 100 个，下面有 **再显示 100 个**；搜索和 **只看存活** 仍会查遍所有笼。在 10 万只小鼠的实验室里，它不到一秒就能打开，以前要 1.6 秒。**首页** 在那里也快了约四分之一，app 启动也更快。
- **A long sheet of fly vials opens faster**: with over 500 vials, in about 0.4 s instead of 0.7.
  - **很长的果蝇管列表打开更快**：超过 500 管时，约 0.4 秒就能打开，以前要 0.7 秒。

### Fixed

- **On a long sheet of vials, Show them** (the vials that ended over 90 days ago) now lists them on every page, not only the first.
  - **很长的果蝇管列表里，显示它们**（90 天前就已结束的管）现在每一页都会列出，不再只有第一页。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.7.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes in this version. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
