## BioManager 2.2.0

### New

- **Paper notes.** A notebook page can now be a **Reading** page: a paper, with its **Authors**, **Journal**, **Year**, **DOI**, **Link** and **Zotero** link as rows under the title. Paste a DOI, a link, or the paper copied from Zotero (BibTeX, RIS or CSL JSON) into **Fill in**, or into **Paper** when you make the page, and they are filled in, title too. **Open in Zotero** opens the paper in Zotero.
  - **论文笔记。** 实验记录本的页面现在可以是 **文献** 页面：一篇论文，标题下按行列出 **作者**、**期刊**、**年份**、**DOI**、**链接** 和 **Zotero** 链接。把 DOI、链接或从 Zotero 复制的论文（BibTeX、RIS 或 CSL JSON）粘贴到 **填写** 里，或新建页面时粘贴到 **论文** 里，这些信息连同标题都会自动填好。**在 Zotero 中打开** 会在 Zotero 里打开这篇论文。
- **Paper notes straight from Zotero.** **Zotero** on the **Reading** page connects your Zotero library (or a lab group's) with a read-only key from zotero.org. Pick a collection, and each paper you add to it becomes a paper note the next time you open the notebook, with its details, abstract and tags filled in. A paper gets one note, ever. Zotero has to sync to zotero.org, and the computer or server running BioManager has to reach it.
  - **从 Zotero 自动生成论文笔记。** **文献** 页面上的 **Zotero** 用 zotero.org 的只读密钥连接你（或实验室群组）的 Zotero 文献库。选一个分类，之后你添加进去的每篇论文都会在你下次打开实验记录本时变成一篇论文笔记，各项信息、摘要和标签都已填好。每篇论文只生成一篇笔记。Zotero 需要同步到 zotero.org，运行 BioManager 的电脑或服务器也需要能访问它。
- **Reading** in the notebook's sidebar lists every paper and talk you can open as cards with their covers, or as every picture in them, so you can find a paper by a figure you remember. **Seminar** pages keep the speaker and their affiliation and are listed there too.
  - 实验记录本侧边栏里的 **文献** 以带封面的卡片列出你能打开的所有论文和讲座，也可以改为显示其中所有的图片，凭记得的一张图就能找到论文。**讲座** 页面会记下报告人和单位，也列在这里。
- **A cover** for any page: **Add cover** puts a picture across the top, uploaded or picked from the page's own pictures.
  - 任何页面都可以加 **封面**：**添加封面** 在页面顶部放一张图片，可以上传，也可以从本页的图片中选。

### Works differently

- **More room for the page.** The notebook's sidebar hides with its button or Ctrl+\ (⌘\ on a Mac), and the page takes the window.
  - **页面更宽。** 实验记录本的侧边栏可以用它的按钮或 Ctrl+\（Mac 上是 ⌘\）收起，页面占满窗口。
- **Topics are easier to manage.** **+** beside *Topics* names a new one in place, a topic's **•••** renames or deletes it, and a page dragged onto a topic moves there.
  - **主题更好管理。** *主题* 旁边的 **+** 直接新建主题，主题的 **•••** 可以重命名或删除，把页面拖到某个主题上就移到那里。
- **New page asks clearly**: the title, the topic, the kind of page (each says what it adds) and what to start from. **Save as template** asks who can use the template.
  - **新建页面问得更清楚**：标题、主题、页面类型（每种都写明它多了什么），以及从什么开始。**另存为模板** 会问谁可以使用这个模板。
- **A long title wraps** instead of being cut off, and stands well above the page's headings. Fewer explaining words around the page.
  - **长标题会换行**，不再被截断，并且明显大于页面里的各级标题。页面周围的说明文字也更少了。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.2.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version adds a table (each person's Zotero connection, with its key encrypted); updating adds it, and keeps everything else as it was. A server that should make paper notes from Zotero has to reach `api.zotero.org`, and one that looks up DOIs `api.crossref.org`. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
