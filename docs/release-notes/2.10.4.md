## BioManager 2.10.4

### New

- **A small animation while a page is loading.** Opening a record, a list or a page, or pressing **Approve** on a proposal, can take a few seconds, which looked as if BioManager had stopped. When a load takes more than a second, one of ten small animations now plays in the page while it comes: a DNA helix, a peptide chain, a cell dividing, a mouse, a zebrafish, a pipette, a well plate and others, picked at random. BioManager learns how long each kind of page takes on your computer, so one that is usually slow shows it at once and a quick one never flashes it. Downloads and links to other sites don't show it.
  - **页面加载时会显示一个小动画。** 打开记录、列表或页面，或在提议上按 **批准**，可能要等几秒，看起来像是 BioManager 卡住了。现在加载超过一秒时，页面里会随机播放十个小动画之一：DNA 双螺旋、肽链、分裂的细胞、小鼠、斑马鱼、移液枪、孔板等。BioManager 会记住在你的电脑上每种页面要加载多久，通常较慢的页面会立刻显示，很快的页面不会闪一下。下载和打开其他网站时不会显示。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.10.4, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
