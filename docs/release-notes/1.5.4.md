## BioManager 1.5.4

### Fixed

- **In the desktop app, Open the lab opens your lab.** After **Find it** found the lab's server, **Open the lab** did nothing, and pressing it again went back to **Open your lab**. The window was refusing to follow a form on to another address. It now goes on to the server. **Move this lab to a server**, **Give the master copy back** and opening the linked lab in this window (**Settings → Devices & copies**) had the same trouble and are mended with it.
  - 桌面版里，“查找”找到实验室服务器后，按“打开实验室”没有反应，再按一次会回到上一页；现在会直接打开。“把这个实验室迁移到服务器”等几个按钮也一起修好了。
  - If you met this on 1.5.3: quit the app and open it again, and it opens your lab; the first press had already remembered it.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.5.4, or download it below. A Mac app on 1.5.2 or earlier may not offer it (fixed in 1.5.3): download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing on a lab server changes from 1.5.3, and nothing in the database. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
