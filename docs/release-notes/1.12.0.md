## BioManager 1.12.0

### New

- **Other measures beside an experiment's main readout.** A plate can now fill another measure, such as an IL-6 ELISA in pg/mL next to tumour volume: in **Plate layout**, the **Readout** list offers the main readout, the experiment's other measures, or **Another measure…** (a name and a unit). Each measure has its own button on the experiment's sheet, where its cells can be typed or corrected, and the chart and **Tests by day** follow the one chosen. **Export** gives each measure its own sheet, and AI assistants see them in the experiment's summary. The main readout stays the one the plan, bench mode and the main readout's day are about.
  - **实验的主要观测指标之外，还可以有其他指标。** 一块板现在可以填进另一个指标，比如肿瘤体积之外的 IL-6 ELISA（pg/mL）：在 **孔板布局** 里，**观测指标** 列表提供主要指标、实验已有的其他指标，或 **其他指标…**（填名称和单位）。每个指标在实验表格上都有自己的按钮，可以在那里输入或修改数值，图表和 **逐日检验** 跟随所选的指标。**导出** 会给每个指标单独一张表，AI 助手也能在实验摘要里看到它们。计划、实验台模式和主要指标的那一天仍然以主要指标为准。
- **Standard curves: Linear, Log–log or 4PL.** Reading a plate with standards, the **Curve** list chooses the fit: the four-parameter logistic usual for ELISA is the default when there are five or more standards over at least a tenfold range. The dialog draws the curve beside the plate, with its equation and r². A fit that doesn't hold is refused in words, and a signal past the curve's top or bottom says to dilute and read again, or that it is less than the assay detects, instead of giving a number.
  - **标准曲线：线性、双对数或 4PL。** 读取带标准品的板时，用 **曲线** 列表选择拟合方式：有五个或更多标准品、浓度跨度至少十倍时，默认用 ELISA 常用的四参数逻辑（4PL）拟合。对话框会在板旁边画出曲线，并显示公式和 r²。拟合不成立时会直接说明原因；信号超出曲线顶端或底端时，会提示稀释后重读，或低于检测下限，而不是给出一个数值。

- **Samples by type.** Samples has a tab for each type above the sheet: **All**, then **tissue**, **blood**, **serum**, **DNA**, **RNA**, **protein** and the rest. A tab lists that type with only its own columns and the boxes that hold it: tissue has the new **Tissue**, **Fixation** and **Orientation**, DNA and RNA the concentration, 260/280, 260/230 and volume. A new sample from a tab is of that type, and its dialog asks only for that type's columns. **+** at the end of the tabs adds a type, and **Used for** in **Configure** sets which columns each type has; any inventory can turn on **A tab for each type**. Every sample also has **Genotype**, which, left empty, shows the source mouse's from the colony, and **Condition**.
  - **按类型管理样本。** 样本库在表格上方为每种类型设了一个标签页：**全部**，然后是 **组织**、**血液**、**血清**、**DNA**、**RNA**、**蛋白** 等。每个标签页只列出该类型的样本，只显示它自己的列和装着它的冻存盒：组织有新增的 **组织**、**固定方式** 和 **切面方向**，DNA 和 RNA 有浓度、260/280、260/230 和体积。在某个标签页里新建的样本就是那种类型，对话框也只问该类型的列。标签页最后的 **+** 用来添加类型，**配置** 里的 **用于** 决定每种类型有哪些列；任何库存都可以勾选 **每种类型一个标签页**。每个样本还有 **基因型**（留空时显示来源小鼠在小鼠群里的基因型）和 **处理条件**。
- **Split notes.** Samples brought in from another database with everything in the notes (*Tissue: Lung; Fixation: 4% PFA; control 2w*): tick them and **Split notes** moves each *Column: value* into the column of that name, leaves the rest in the notes, and lists any name with no column. **Batch history** undoes it.
  - **拆分备注。** 从别的数据库导进来、所有内容都在备注里的样本（*Tissue: Lung; Fixation: 4% PFA; control 2w*）：勾选后点 **拆分备注**，每个“列名: 值”会移到同名的列，其余留在备注里，没有对应列的名称会列出来。**批量操作记录** 可以撤销。

### Works differently

- **One Undo takes a whole plate back.** Saving a plate's readings is one entry in **Batch history**: its **Undo** removes the plate and its readings, and puts back any readings the plate replaced.
  - **一次撤销就能撤回整块板。** 保存一块板的读数在 **批量操作记录** 里是一条：它的 **撤销** 会删除这块板和它的读数，并恢复被这块板替换的读数。
- **The notebook's experiment block shows each day's reagent lot,** marked "(expired)" when it had expired on the day it was used, as the experiment page does.
  - **笔记本里的实验块会显示每一天所用试剂的批号**，用的时候已经过期的会标上“（已过期）”，和实验页面一样。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.12.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database's tables; Samples databases get their type tabs and the new columns when it starts. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
