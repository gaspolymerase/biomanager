## BioManager 1.2.2

### Fixed

- **The language you choose now sticks** ([#40](https://github.com/gaspolymerase/biomanager/issues/40)).
  - 中文 / English on the sign-in page went back to the browser's language as soon as you signed in. It now carries on, and becomes your choice when you haven't chosen one in **Settings → Language**.
  - On a Mac set to Chinese, the desktop app showed English: its window told BioManager the Mac was English. The app now says it speaks Chinese, and its window follows the computer's language when you haven't chosen one.

### What works differently

- **Settings → Language** changes as soon as you pick a language; there is no **Save** button any more.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.2.2, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server**:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.

  Nothing in the database changes from 1.2.1. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
