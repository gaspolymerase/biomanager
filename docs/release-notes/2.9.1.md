## BioManager 2.9.1

### Fixed

- **Set field offers a column's choices again**, and its list stays inside the window. Picking a column with choices (a sample's **Fixation**: 4% PFA, formalin…) lists them under **value** as you type; a long list opens over the field when there is more room there, and scrolls rather than running past the window's edge.
  - **设置字段又能列出这一列的选项了**，而且选项列表不会超出窗口。选一个有选项的列（样本的 **固定方式**：4% PFA、福尔马林……），在 **值** 里输入时就会列出这些选项；列表很长时，哪边空间大就往哪边开，放不下就在列表里滚动，不会超出窗口边缘。
- **Every field with suggestions** keeps its list inside the window, in a short window or low on the page.
  - **所有带建议的输入框**，在窗口较矮或位于页面下方时，建议列表都不会超出窗口。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.9.1, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
