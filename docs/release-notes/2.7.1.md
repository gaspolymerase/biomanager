## BioManager 2.7.1

### Works differently

- **Home counts the active mice only**, and how many of them are yours. Its tiles keep what is **Due this week** apart from what is **Overdue**.
  - **首页只统计活跃的小鼠**，以及其中有多少是你的。磁贴把 **本周到期** 和 **已逾期** 分开显示。
- **A big lab opens faster.** In a lab of 100,000 mice: a long sheet of plants opens in 1.3 s instead of 4.8, and its **Show them** in under a second instead of 8.7 s; plasmids in about half a second, also in a lab with its own plasmid columns (4–6 s before); **Reagents** in 0.4 s instead of 0.9; **Orders** loads a third of the page it did, as its **Board** comes when you open it; and **Home** and the cage sheet are quicker too.
  - **大型实验室打开更快。** 在 10 万只小鼠的实验室里：很长的植物列表 1.3 秒就能打开，以前要 4.8 秒，它的 **显示它们** 不到一秒，以前要 8.7 秒；质粒约半秒，有自定义质粒列的实验室也一样（以前要 4–6 秒）；**试剂** 0.4 秒，以前要 0.9 秒；**订单** 页只有原来的三分之一大，因为它的 **看板** 在打开时才加载；**首页** 和笼表也更快了。
- **A plant or other animal that ended over 90 days ago** waits behind **Show them** on a long sheet, as the fly vials do.
  - 在很长的列表里，**90 天前就已结束的植物或其他动物** 放在 **显示它们** 后面，和果蝇管一样。
- **A zebrafish tank of the lab's breeding stock is a breeder tank**, as a mouse cage of it is a **Breeder** cage, and is shared with the lab. Tanks you had as *breeding* become *breeder*, and whoever could edit them still can. A tank being crossed is still a *mating* tank.
  - **存放实验室种鱼的斑马鱼缸叫种鱼缸**，和小鼠的 **种鼠笼** 一样，与实验室共享。用途原为 *breeding* 的鱼缸改为 *breeder*，原来能编辑它的人仍然可以编辑。正在配对的鱼缸仍是 *mating*。

### Fixed

- **Already on order** no longer counts an order made with **Order again** from reagent #1 as on order for #12.
  - **已在订购** 不会再把从 #1 号试剂 **再次订购** 的订单算作 #12 号的订单。
- **A facility manager** can change the settings of an inventory, a fly database or a worm database, as their role says.
  - **设施管理员** 可以修改库存、果蝇数据库和线虫数据库的设置，与该角色的说明一致。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.7.1, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  The database changes once, by itself, when the new version starts: zebrafish tanks marked *breeding* are marked *breeder*. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
