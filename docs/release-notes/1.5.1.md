## BioManager 1.5.1

### Works differently

- **In Chinese, a mouse cage's purposes read alike: 种鼠笼 · 配种笼 · 实验笼** (Breeder, Breeding, Experiment), in the Purpose column, on the cards, in the buttons above the cages and under **Configure → Cage purposes**. Breeding was 繁殖, which said breeding in general rather than a cage mating now.
  - 中文界面里，笼的三种用途统一为 **种鼠笼 · 配种笼 · 实验笼**。“配种笼”指正在交配的笼，只有它有“产仔”“基因型鉴定”和“断奶”。
- The **Breeders** tab's heading in Chinese is 实验室的种鼠.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.5.1, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes from 1.5.0. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
