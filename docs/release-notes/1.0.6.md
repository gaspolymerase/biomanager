## BioManager 1.0.6

### New

- **BioManager in Chinese (中文).** Choose **Settings → Language**, or 中文 / English on the sign-in page. Without a choice, BioManager follows the language your browser or computer asks for first.
  - Translated so far: the sidebar and menus, Home, the mouse colony with its cage panel and dialogs, the sheets, the calendar, cage cards and Settings.
  - Still in English: the other databases' pages, and the messages BioManager shows after you save or change something. They are next.
  - What your lab typed (names, notes, strains) always stays as typed.
- The website is in Chinese too: **[biomanager.org/zh/](https://biomanager.org/zh/)**, with the user guide and the server page.

### Fixed

- **Windows set to Chinese, Japanese or Korean**: the desktop app starts again. Since 0.10.2 it closed at once, before any window opened, on Windows 10 and 11 alike.
  - If BioManager never opened for you on Windows, download 1.0.6 below. Your data was never touched.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.0.6, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and a copy is taken before the database is upgraded.
  - On a Chinese, Japanese or Korean Windows, where 1.0.5 doesn't start, download 1.0.6 below, unpack the whole folder and open `BioManager.exe` in it.
- **Lab server**:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.

  Nothing in the database changes in this release. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
