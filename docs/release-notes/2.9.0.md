## BioManager 2.9.0

### New

- **A new look, and a Solid one beside it.** The wallpaper now covers the whole window, sheets are panes of glass with a frosted toolbar and header, and the folded sidebar's icons are larger and darker. Prefer no glass? **Settings → Appearance & language → Look → Solid** gives white sheets on plain paper instead, for you alone.
  - **新外观，以及可选的实色版。** 背景现在铺满整个窗口，表格是一块玻璃，工具栏和表头是磨砂的；收起的侧栏图标更大、更深。不喜欢玻璃效果？**设置 → 外观与语言 → 外观样式 → 实色** 换成素色底上的白色表格，只对你自己生效。
- **The Inspector is the record's own panel.** Beside a sheet it shows the row's name, its labels (status, sex, shared) and **Open**; ↑ ↓ go to the row above or below; **Overview** has its fields and, for a mouse or a cage, its rack; **History** lists the changes to it, who made each and when.
  - **检查器成为记录自己的面板。** 表格旁显示这一行的名字、标签（状态、性别、共享）和 **打开**；↑ ↓ 查看上一行或下一行；**概览** 是它的字段，小鼠和笼子还有所在的架子；**历史** 列出它的改动、谁改的、什么时候改的。
- **Forms that are quicker to fill in.** A mouse's or a cage's form slides in at the right with the sheet beside it. Sex is a row of buttons, transgenes are added one at a time, and choosing a rack draws it: click a free place for the position, or a cage to put the mouse in it. **⌘/Ctrl ↵** saves any form.
  - **更好填的表单。** 小鼠和笼子的表单从右侧滑出，表格仍在旁边。性别是一排按钮，转基因一个一个添加；选了架子就画出这个架子：点空位填入位置，或点一个笼子把小鼠放进去。**⌘/Ctrl ↵** 保存任何表单。
- **Utilities answers a sum as you type it.** Type *10 mM to 50 µM in 1 mL* or *1:1000 in 5 mL* in its search and the answer shows under it; *4000 rpm* opens rpm ↔ × g. Every tool is a tile saying what it works out, with buttons by kind; pinned tools show their last answer, and each tool keeps your **Recent sums**.
  - **工具页边输入边算。** 在搜索框输入 *10 mM to 50 µM in 1 mL* 或 *1:1000 in 5 mL*，下方直接显示结果；*4000 rpm* 打开 rpm ↔ × g。每个工具是一块写着它算什么的卡片，可按类别筛选；固定的工具显示上次的结果，每个工具保留 **最近算过** 的记录。
- **Experiments fit each database's animals.** Plants measure germination, height, rosette area, days to flowering and leaf number, and are treated, sprayed, watered and inoculated. Every animal shows its **Age on day 1** (weeks for a mouse, dpf for a fish, days after sowing for a plant). **Randomize** shares the animals into groups, balanced by sex, housing and weight, and seeded. A survival readout is also tested over the whole experiment by the log-rank test.
  - **实验按各数据库的动物适配。** 植物测发芽率、高度、莲座面积、开花天数和叶片数，处理方式有喷施、浇水、接种等。每个个体显示 **第 1 天时的年龄**（小鼠按周、鱼按 dpf、植物按播种后天数）。**随机分组** 按性别、饲养单元和体重均衡分组，并给出随机种子。存活类指标还会对整个实验做 log-rank 检验。

### Works differently

- Vials and plates take how many each started with when added, and **Fill in** beside **At start** gives every one the same count. An organism's weights and lengths typed in an experiment are kept on the animal too, under **Measurements** in its panel; with **Body weights** on in its database's **Configure**, a fall from its first weight shows under **Losing weight**, as a mouse's does.
  - 果蝇管和培养皿添加时可以填每个开始时有多少只，**开始时** 旁边的 **填写** 给每一个填同一个数。在实验里输入的其他物种的体重和长度也会记到个体自己身上（面板里的 **测量记录**）；数据库在 **配置** 里打开 **体重** 后，比第一次体重下降时会像小鼠一样列在 **体重下降** 里。
- The assistant can add a rack of vials or plates, or single fish, clutches, vials, animals or cohorts, to an experiment.
  - AI 助手可以把一整架果蝇管或培养皿，或单条鱼、鱼卵、果蝇管、个体或群体加到实验里。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.9.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
