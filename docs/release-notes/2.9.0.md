## BioManager 2.9.0

### New

- **Find a plasmid by what is on its map.** Searching the plasmids sheet also lists, under the search, the plasmids that carry what you typed as a feature on their map (*WPRE*, *GCaMP*), whatever their Insert says.
  - **按图谱上的元件找质粒。** 在质粒表格里搜索时，搜索框下面也会列出图谱上带有这个元件的质粒（如 *WPRE*、*GCaMP*），不管插入片段写的是什么。
- **An AI assistant can read what orders cost and the lab's vendors**, with the new `spending` and `vendors` tools. The BioManager plugin is 1.8.0.
  - **AI 助手可以读取订单花费和供应商**，新增 `spending` 和 `vendors` 两个工具。BioManager 插件为 1.8.0。
- **A record's dialog shows what the lab keeps about its vendor** beside **Vendor**: website, customer number and contact, with **Open in Vendors**.
  - **记录的对话框会在 供应商 旁显示实验室记下的信息**：网站、客户编号和联系方式，并有 **在供应商页面打开**。

### Works differently

- **Spending counts an order in the month it was ordered**, not the month it was asked for; an order still waiting counts in the month it was asked for.
  - **花费按订购的月份计入订单**，而不是申请的月份；还在等待订购的订单仍按申请的月份计入。
- **Admins are told when an order takes someone past a spending limit**, once, under *Orders* notifications.
  - **订单让某人超出花费限额时，管理员会收到一次通知**（在“订购”通知下）。
- **Spending and Vendors fit a phone**: the choices wrap and the tables keep the columns that matter.
  - **花费和供应商页面适配手机**：选项会换行，表格只保留关键的列。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.9.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes in this version. Orders placed before it have no ordered date, so Spending counts them in the month they were asked for. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
