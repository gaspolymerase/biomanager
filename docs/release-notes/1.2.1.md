## BioManager 1.2.1

### New

- **Samples show the source mouse's Custom tag as a column**, right after **Source**: however the mouse is marked (an ear tag, an ear punch, a tail tattoo), read straight from the mouse colony, so re-tagging a mouse there updates every sample of it at once. If you hide **Custom tag** under **Columns** on the Mice tab, it goes from Samples too, for you.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.2.1, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server**:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.

  Nothing in the database changes from 1.2.0. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
