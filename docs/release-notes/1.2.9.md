## BioManager 1.2.9

### Fixed

- **中文 works in the desktop app** ([#40](https://github.com/gaspolymerase/biomanager/issues/40)).
  - The downloaded app (Mac, Windows and Linux) was built without its Chinese translations, so choosing 中文, on the sign-in page or in **Settings → Appearance & language**, was saved but every page stayed in English. It had been so since Chinese arrived in 1.1.
  - Lab servers were never affected.
  - 桌面版现在可以显示中文了：之前的安装包漏掉了中文翻译文件，无论在登录页还是「设置 → 外观与语言」里选了中文，页面都只显示英文。实验室服务器不受影响。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.2.9, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and the language you chose before shows at once.
- **Lab server**: nothing changes for a server; update when convenient (**Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now** on 1.2.6 or later).

  Nothing in the database changes from 1.2.8.
