## BioManager 2.0.1

### Works differently

- **The inventory sheets open about twice as fast.** Each row used to carry every menu's whole list (Kind, Status, Box, Stored at, Hazard) and, on Samples, every mouse ID of the colony. Now a row holds what it shows, and a menu's list comes when you open it. In a lab with about 5,000 reagents, in Chrome: 23 s before, 11 s now; in a lab of 4,000 mice, Reagents and Samples open in under 2 s instead of nearly 3 s. Nothing to change in how you work: click a cell and type, as before.
  - **库存表打开快了约一倍。** 以前每一行都带着每个菜单的完整列表（类型、状态、盒子、存放温度、危险性），样本表还带着鼠群里所有小鼠的编号。现在每一行只带它显示的内容，菜单的列表在你打开时才载入。在有约 5,000 条试剂的实验室里，用 Chrome：以前 23 秒，现在 11 秒；在有 4,000 只小鼠的实验室里，试剂和样本表从将近 3 秒变成不到 2 秒。用法不变：点一个单元格直接输入，和以前一样。
- **The Breeders tab** took 40 s to open in a colony with years of history. Like the other colony tabs, it now leaves out Breeder cages left empty for over 90 days, and mice in them that ended that long ago; a line at the top says how many, and **Show them** lists them. It opens in about 2 s.
  - **种鼠** 标签页在有多年历史的鼠群里要 40 秒才打开。现在它和鼠群的其他标签页一样，不列出空置超过 90 天的种鼠笼，以及笼里 90 天前就已结束的小鼠；顶部有一行说明有多少，**显示它们** 会把它们列出来。大约 2 秒就能打开。
- **Every page draws its icons with less work**, which a long sheet notices most.
  - **每个页面画图标的工作更少了**，长表格最能感觉到。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.0.1, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database's tables. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
