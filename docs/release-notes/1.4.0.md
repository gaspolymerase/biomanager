## BioManager 1.4.0

### New

- **A new look, after Apple's Liquid Glass.** The sidebar, the row of tabs and each page's buttons float as frosted glass over the page, which scrolls beneath them; the records themselves sit on solid ground, so they stay easy to read.
  - The selected tab, or view (Mice / Cages / Litters, My colony / Shared / Everyone), slides to what you pick. Buttons give a little when pressed, and menus grow out of the button that opened them.
  - Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion in your computer's settings are honoured.
- **No title bar on a Mac.** The desktop window's close, minimise and zoom buttons sit at the top of the sidebar, beside your tabs. Drag the empty part of the top row to move the window; double-click it to zoom.
- **Easier to read.** Text is set in Inter, which tells I, l and 1 apart in IDs and genotypes and looks the same on every computer, with your computer's own Chinese font.
  - **Settings → Appearance & language → Font** switches to the computer's font (SF Pro on a Mac, Segoe UI on Windows).
  - **Text size** there makes everything smaller or larger in the Mac app; in a browser, its zoom does the same.
- **On a phone, a tab bar at the bottom**: **Home**, **Calendar**, **Databases**, **Notebook** and **Scan**, with **Search** in the round button beside it. It shrinks while you read down a page.
  - **Databases** lists the lab's databases.
  - **Scan** reads a cage, tank or vial card with the Android or iPhone app's scanner, or with the phone's camera; in Safari on an iPhone it reminds you to point the Camera app at the card.
- **The way in.** The front page is the sign-in to your lab, with the lab's name and its databases beside it.
  - **Remember me on this computer** offers your account by name next time.
  - **Ask to join** tells every admin, and the page changes the moment one approves. A forgotten password, or no guest code, asks the lab's admins.
  - The desktop app first asks whether your lab already uses BioManager: **Yes** opens your lab's address, **No** starts a new lab.
- **Save the whole lab** (**Settings → Your data**): records, accounts and uploaded files in one `.biomanager` file. A new server takes it with **Bring a lab from the desktop app**, and the desktop app can **Move this lab to a server** (**Settings → Devices & copies**), keeping its own copy, read only, with a way back.
- **Start a new lab** and **Bring back a lab set aside**, each undoing the other (the first page, the sign-in, and **Settings → Lab**). On a server, `deploy/backup/start-over.sh` sets the lab aside and `--undo` puts it back.

### Works differently

- **The calendar's month is its title.** Back, Today and Forward, the Day / Week / Month / List switch and **New event** are in the row at the top; the small month and the layers sit beside the grid without a card.
- **A notebook page is a sheet of paper.** Its path and tools (**Share**, **Comments**, **Sign**, **History**) are in the row at the top. Comments, History, Share and Signatures open on the right while the page list folds away, and the formatting bar floats over the page.
- **A sheet's toolbar keeps to one line.** **Print** and **Import from Excel** are under **•••** beside **New**; on a narrow window the other buttons show their icons, with their names in the tooltip.
- Labels and column headings are in sentence case, not capitals, so English and Chinese read alike.
- Dialogs, menus, the ⌘K search and the messages at the bottom of the window have the new look.
- The iPhone and Android apps hide their own Scan button on pages that have the phone's tab bar.

### Fixed

- The desktop app's **Go** menu lists your databases and pages again; it had stayed empty.
- A link to a section of Settings no longer scrolls it up under the top of the window.
- A lab file holding a record that points at one it doesn't have is refused before anything is written, with the table and row named.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.4.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Android app**: download `BioManager-Android.apk` below for **Scan** in the tab bar.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
