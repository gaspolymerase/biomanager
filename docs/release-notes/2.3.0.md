## BioManager 2.3.0

### New

- **A new look, the same everywhere.** Every dialog now has its title and **×** at the top and **Cancel** beside one main button at the bottom; on a phone it slides up from the bottom of the screen. Every dropdown, field, menu and message is drawn the same way across the app, in light and dark mode.
  - **全新外观，处处一致。** 每个对话框都是标题和 **×** 在顶部，**取消** 和一个主按钮在底部；在手机上从屏幕底部滑出。整个应用里的下拉框、输入框、菜单和提示都用同一种样式，浅色和深色模式都是如此。
- **Suggestions in the app's own list.** A field that suggests what the lab has typed before (owner, strain, cage…) now opens a list in the app instead of the browser's: typing narrows it, ↓ and Enter pick one, Esc closes it. In a sheet ↓ still moves down a row, so Alt+↓ opens the list there.
  - **建议列表在应用里显示。** 会提示实验室以前输入过的内容的输入框（负责人、品系、笼……）现在在应用里打开列表，不再用浏览器自带的：输入会缩小列表，↓ 和回车选中一项，Esc 关闭。在表格里 ↓ 仍是移到下一行，所以要用 Alt+↓ 打开列表。
- **Topics inside topics.** In the notebook, a topic can hold other topics, as folders do, and has an icon. A topic's name opens its own page: the topics in it as tiles and its pages as cards or a list. The notebook opens on **Home**: your **Favorites**, the pages you edited last and your topics. Star a page to keep it in **Favorites**, and drag pages and topics in the sidebar to move, reorder and nest them. **Inbox** leaves out protocols, papers, talks and meeting notes, which **Protocols**, **Reading** and **Meetings** list.
  - **主题里可以有主题。** 实验记录本里，主题可以像文件夹一样包含其他主题，还有自己的图标。点主题名打开它自己的页面：里面的主题以图块显示，页面以卡片或列表显示。实验记录本打开时显示 **首页**：你的 **收藏夹**、最近编辑的页面和你的主题。给页面加星就会留在 **收藏夹** 里；在侧边栏里拖动页面和主题可以移动、排序和嵌套。**收件箱** 不再列出实验流程、论文、讲座和会议记录，它们分别在 **实验流程**、**文献** 和 **会议** 里。
- **The calendar in your Mac's Calendar.** In the Mac app, **Calendar → Mac Calendar** keeps BioManager's events, to-dos and what is due in a calendar called *BioManager* in one of your Mac Calendar's accounts (iCloud, Google, Exchange or On My Mac), so they reach your phone and Google Calendar with no lab server.
  - **在 Mac 的日历里看日程。** 在 Mac 应用里，**日历 → Mac 日历** 会把 BioManager 的活动、待办和到期事项放进你 Mac 日历某个账户（iCloud、Google、Exchange 或“我的 Mac”）里一个名为 *BioManager* 的日历，不需要实验室服务器，就能同步到手机和 Google 日历。
- **Feedback reaches BioManager's makers.** **Send feedback** has **Also send this to BioManager's makers** (ticked in the desktop app, unticked on a lab server), with an optional way to reach you. It sends your note, the page and the version, never your name or your records.
  - **反馈能送到 BioManager 的开发者手里。** **发送反馈** 里有 **同时发给 BioManager 的开发者**（桌面应用默认勾选，实验室服务器默认不勾），还可以留下联系方式。发送的是你的留言、所在页面和版本，绝不包括你的名字或记录。
- **A design gallery** for admins (**Settings → Version → Design gallery**): every control, dialog, menu and pill on one page, for checking how a change looks.
  - 给管理员的 **设计样例**（**设置 → 版本 → 设计样例**）：应用里的每种控件、对话框、菜单和标签都在一页上，用来检查外观上的改动。

### Works differently

- **Home has three views**, as tabs under the greeting: **Overview** (your cards), **Timeline** (the coming weeks, a row per kind of work) and **By place** (your racks from above, with the jobs numbered in walking order). They were Classic, Tracks and Freezer; your choice is kept. **Customize** sits beside the tabs on Overview, and *Your databases* is now one of the cards to add.
  - **首页有三种视图**，就是问候语下方的标签：**概览**（你的卡片）、**时间线**（未来几周，每类工作一行）和 **按位置**（从上方俯视你的笼架，工作按走过去的顺序编号）。它们原来叫经典、时间轴和冰箱；你的选择会保留。**自定义** 在概览的标签旁边，*你的数据库* 现在是可以添加的卡片之一。
- **Each database has a tab bar** under its title, with its counts or **My colony · Shared · Everyone** beside the tabs. Help that sat on the page as a paragraph is behind an **ⓘ**.
  - **每个数据库的标题下都有一排标签**，计数或 **我的鼠群 · 共享 · 全实验室** 在标签旁边。原来一直显示在页面上的说明段落，现在收在 **ⓘ** 里。
- **The cage sheet fits a laptop screen.** *Card ID* and the cage's *Note* start hidden; tick them under **Columns**. Search finds them either way.
  - **笼表格在笔记本电脑屏幕上放得下。** *笼卡编号* 和笼的 *备注* 默认隐藏，在 **列** 里勾选即可显示。不论是否显示，搜索都能找到它们。
- **A cage's purpose has its own colour**: Breeding teal, Breeder green, Experiment blue, and the lab's own purposes the next colours in its list. A person is a round badge with their initials.
  - **笼的用途有各自的颜色**：配种笼青绿、种鼠笼绿色、实验笼蓝色，实验室自己加的用途按列表顺序依次着色。每个人显示为带首字母的圆形标记。
- **On a phone**, a sheet's **New** is a round **+** beside its other buttons, and fields no longer make the iPhone zoom in.
  - **在手机上**，表格的 **新建** 是其他按钮旁边的圆形 **+**，输入框也不会再让 iPhone 放大页面。
- **Giving a record away** follows one rule in every database: its owner, an admin, or animal care for the animals they look after can, and only to a lab member.
  - **把记录转给别人** 在每个数据库里都遵循同一条规则：记录的负责人、管理员，或负责照看这些动物的动物护理人员可以转，而且只能转给实验室成员。

### Fixed

- Anyone who could edit a shared breeding tank could give it away, to any name.
  - 任何能编辑共享繁殖缸的人都能把它转给别人，而且可以转给任意名字。
- A **Litter born** date typed on a cage that isn't a Breeding cage made a litter that every litter button then refused. Only a Breeding cage takes one now.
  - 在非配种笼上输入 **产仔** 日期，会生成一窝之后所有产仔相关按钮都无法处理的幼鼠。现在只有配种笼能记录产仔。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.3.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version adds a table (each person's notebook favourites) and two columns (a notebook topic's parent and icon); updating adds them, and keeps everything else as it was. A server whose admins want feedback to reach BioManager's makers has to reach `us.i.posthog.com`, as the daily counts do. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
