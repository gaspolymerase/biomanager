## BioManager 1.2.0

### New

- **Chemicals**: a new database for the lab's chemical list, kept apart from Reagents: name, **Abbreviation**, **CAS**, **MW (g/mol)** and **Purity (%)**, with the formula, density, lot, expiry and where each bottle is. Bring your Excel sheet in with **Import from Excel**. `@` in a notebook page finds a chemical by its abbreviation or CAS number, and the calculators in **Utilities** know each molecular weight. Add it from **Add database**; Lab setup offers it too.
- **Formulation** in the notebook (type `/formulation` in a page): what goes into a reaction or a batch, by mass.
  - Type a chemical's name, abbreviation or CAS number and pick it from Chemicals; its molecular weight, purity, density and lot come with it.
  - Type the mass you weigh and **Amount** gives the moles, allowing for purity. Or mark one row as the basis and give the others in equivalents: the mass to weigh follows, and changes when the basis does.
  - Each row shows its weight %, a liquid its volume, and the total is underneath. The chemical lists the page under **Used in notebook pages**.
- **Columns of your own on the mouse colony, zebrafish and plasmids**, as the other databases have always had: open the database, press **Configure**, and add them under **Your own columns** (an organ weight, a perfusion yes / no, a date, a lab member). They work like the built-in ones: edited in the sheet, set on many rows at once with **Set field**, matched by name in **Import from Excel**, and found by search.
- **Ear tag** on every mouse, beside its number: whatever is on the animal itself (a tag number, an ear punch such as RF or LB, a tattoo). Search finds a mouse by it, a spreadsheet column called *ear tag*, *notch* or *tattoo* imports into it, pointing at a mouse's number shows it, and a sample shows its mouse's ear tag beside the mouse.
- **Card ID** on cages: the number on the animal facility's own cage card, as a column of the cage sheet, searched like the rest.
- **Arrange the sidebar**: **All databases → Order** puts the lab's databases in the order you work in; drag one by its grip, or use the arrows, then **Save the order**. An admin arranges it, for everyone.
- **Samples**: a sample's **Source** is edited in the sheet like any other cell, and **Set field** sets the Source of many samples at once, so one mouse's whole harvest takes it in one go.

### What works differently

- A **log pasted into a data sheet** with its headers (time down the first column, readings such as temperature and pressure beside it) is drawn as a line over time; **Y** picks the reading.
- A cage's mice show the transgene columns the **Mice** tab shows, so *Transgene 3* and *4* appear in the cages when they are in use. The cage sheet's columns were rearranged for Card ID, so any column choices you made there start afresh.
- A new experiment page ends with **Summary & next steps**.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.2.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and a copy is taken before the database is upgraded.
- **Lab server**:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.

  The database upgrades itself on start, adding a mouse's ear tag and a place for your own columns on mice, tanks, fish and plasmids; every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
