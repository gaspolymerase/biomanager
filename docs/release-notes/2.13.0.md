## BioManager 2.13.0

### New

- **A black-and-white theme, Mono.** In **Settings → Appearance & language**, **Mono** makes BioManager black, white and grey: no glass and no colour, black buttons and a black icon, the system font, and lines between rows. In dark mode it turns around, white buttons on near-black. Red, amber and the green dots keep their colour, so a warning still stands out.
  - **黑白主题：黑白。** 在 **设置 → 外观与语言** 里选 **黑白**，BioManager 就只剩黑、白、灰：没有玻璃效果，也没有颜色，按钮和图标是黑色的，用系统字体，行之间用分隔线。深色模式下反过来，近黑的底上是白色按钮。红色、橙色的提醒和绿色的圆点保留颜色，提醒依然醒目。
- **Mono and Ink on their own.** **Look** has a third choice, **Mono**, which goes with any colour (grey pages with coral buttons, say), and **Colour** has a ninth, **Ink**: black, and white in dark mode.
  - **黑白外观和墨色也可以单独用。** **外观样式** 多了第三个选项 **黑白**，可以和任何颜色搭配（比如灰色的页面配珊瑚色按钮）；**颜色** 多了第九种 **墨色**：浅色模式下是黑色，深色模式下是白色。

### Works differently

- **Bench is no longer a theme.** If you chose it, your choices stay as they were and now show as your own look. **Bench mode** in experiments is unchanged.
  - **实验台不再是一个主题。** 选过它的人，原来的各项设置保持不变，现在算作你自己的搭配。实验里的 **实验台模式** 不受影响。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.13.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
