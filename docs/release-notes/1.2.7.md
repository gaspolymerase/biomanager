## BioManager 1.2.7

### New

- **Protocols, Recipes and Meetings open as pages of their own.** In the notebook's sidebar, each now opens in the page instead of the panel at the side.
  - **Protocols**: search the lab's protocols, open one to read or change it, or make one with **New protocol**. The common protocols built in are below them: open one to read it, and **Copy** it to make it yours. In a page, `/protocol` still opens the protocols beside it to insert one.
  - **Recipes**: open a recipe to change it in the recipe editor, which saves as you go, or make one with **New recipe**. **Save a copy** of a built-in one to make it yours.
  - **Meetings**: each meeting series as a card, with who presents next; **Edit** changes its day, place and presenting order.
- **Folders for protocols and recipes.** **New folder** makes one, with an icon you pick: organs, organisms, lab equipment, procedures and more. Drag a protocol or recipe onto a folder to file it, or pick its folder with the folder button on its card (a protocol also in its own bar). **Edit folder** renames it or changes its icon.
  - Folders are the lab's, shared by everyone. You file the protocols and recipes you can edit; a folder's maker or an admin renames or deletes it, and deleting one keeps what was in it.
  - `/protocol` in a page and **Load from library** in a recipe block list them folder by folder.
  - The guide's [Notebook](https://biomanager.org/guide/notebook.html#experiments-and-protocols) page has the details.

### Works differently

- Icons that were cut off on the right, such as the people, cart, scale and fish, now show whole, and narrow ones sit in the middle of their space instead of to the left.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.2.7, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and a copy is taken before the database is upgraded.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time (it is safe to run again).

  The database upgrades itself on start, adding the folders; every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
