## BioManager 2.0.9

### Works differently

- **Every sheet opens sooner in Chrome, Edge, the Windows app and on Android.** Since 2.0.6 an inventory's cells are drawn as plain text until you click, tap or Tab to them. Now the other sheets do the same: **Mice**, **Cages**, **Litters**, fish and **Tanks**, fly vials, the other organisms and **Plasmids**. They look and work as before: a menu opens its list, a cage's rack and position open together, and pasting a column from a spreadsheet fills the rows below. About 950 mice open in 2.6 seconds instead of 3.2. Safari and the Mac app are unchanged.
  - **在 Chrome、Edge、Windows 版和 Android 上，所有表格都打开得更快。** 从 2.0.6 起，库存表的单元格先显示为普通文字，点击、轻触或按 Tab 进入时才变成输入框。现在其他表格也一样：**小鼠**、**笼**、**窝**、斑马鱼和 **鱼缸**、果蝇管、其他生物以及 **质粒**。外观和用法都和以前一样：菜单会展开选项，笼的笼架和位置一起打开，从表格软件粘贴一列也会依次填入下面的行。约 950 只小鼠的表格从 3.2 秒缩短到 2.6 秒。Safari 和 Mac 版不变。
- **A long mouse, litter, tank or vial sheet comes a page at a time.** As inventories have since 2.0.2, a sheet of more than 500 entries opens with its first page, and each other page comes as you turn to it, in a second or less. Search, sorting, the buttons above the sheet, its count, **Export** and **Print** still go by every entry. About 950 mice now open in 1.2 seconds; in a large lab, 3,300 tanks open in 5.4 seconds instead of 14, and its mice in 1.8 instead of 5.6. The **Cages** sheet still opens whole, as its cards and **Open all** need every cage.
  - **很长的小鼠、窝、鱼缸或果蝇管表格分页载入。** 和 2.0.2 起的库存表一样，超过 500 条记录的表格打开时只有第一页，翻到其他页时再载入那一页，一秒左右。搜索、排序、表格上方的按钮、计数、**导出** 和 **打印** 仍按全部记录来算。约 950 只小鼠的表格现在 1.2 秒打开；在一个大实验室里，3,300 个鱼缸从 14 秒缩短到 5.4 秒，小鼠从 5.6 秒缩短到 1.8 秒。**笼** 表格仍整张打开，因为它的卡片和 **全部展开** 需要每个笼。
- **An inventory's Box grid comes when you switch to it**, so the sheet opens without it, and **Export** of a long inventory takes about half the time (a large one in 5.3 seconds instead of 9.7, and at once a second time).
  - **库存的冻存盒视图在切换到它时才载入**，表格打开时不再带着它；很长的库存表 **导出** 用时约减半（一个大库存从 9.7 秒缩短到 5.3 秒，第二次立即完成）。

### Fixed

- **A mouse's cell changed back now saves.** On **Mice**, changing a cell (such as **Status**), then changing it back to what it was when the page opened, left the second change unsaved until the page was reloaded.
  - **小鼠单元格改回原值现在会保存。** 在 **小鼠** 里，改了一个单元格（例如 **状态**）后再改回页面打开时的值，第二次修改以前不会保存，直到刷新页面。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first. This is the update that matters for this version: the pages come from the server, so a desktop app or phone joined to a lab's server opens sheets faster once the server is on 2.0.9.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.0.9, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database's tables. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
