## BioManager 1.2.5

### New

- **Start a new lab**, on the desktop app's sign-in page, for a lab nobody on this computer can sign in to: the admin's password is lost, or someone else made the first account and you want a lab of your own.
  - The lab, with its accounts, records and files, is set aside in the data folder under `old-labs`, not deleted. The next time BioManager opens, it starts an empty lab, and the first account you create is its admin.
  - Only the person at the computer is offered it: a lab server, or a desktop app shared on the network, doesn't have it.
  - The guide's [People and access](https://biomanager.org/guide/people-and-guests.html#troubleshooting) page says when to use it, and [Restore a backup](https://biomanager.org/guide/restore-a-backup.html) how to bring a set-aside lab back.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.2.5, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server**:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.

  Nothing in the database changes from 1.2.4, and a lab server looks the same: Start a new lab is the desktop app's only. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
