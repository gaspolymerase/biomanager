## BioManager 1.5.2

### New

- **A chemical can have several hazards** ([#58](https://github.com/gaspolymerase/biomanager/issues/58)). **Hazard** in Chemicals and Reagents takes several at once, ticked in a small menu in the sheet or a row of boxes in the dialog, so a bottle can be flammable and an explosive precursor.
  - Beside flammable, corrosive, toxic, oxidiser, irritant and biohazard there are now explosive, highly toxic, and the controlled classes a safety office asks about: drug precursor (易制毒), explosive precursor (易制爆) and narcotic or psychotropic.
  - **Configure** changes the choices or adds the lab's own, and **Several choices** is a column type any database can use.
  - A Hazard field your lab never edited gets the new list when it updates; one you edited keeps its choices. "none" goes, since an empty field already says it.
  - 化学品和试剂的「危险性」可以多选，并加入了易制毒、易制爆、剧毒、易爆、麻醉或精神药品；在「配置」里可以修改或增加类别。

### Fixed

- **The notebook's / menu, Insert menu and toolbar are in Chinese** ([#61](https://github.com/gaspolymerase/biomanager/issues/61)), and find things by their Chinese names (`/链接` finds Link to page).
  - 实验记录本的斜杠菜单、插入菜单和工具栏都变成中文了，也可以用中文搜索。
- **Plasmid editor** ([#57](https://github.com/gaspolymerase/biomanager/issues/57)):
  - **Export** and **Import** showed each other's icon.
  - In the Mac app the sequence's letters filled about two-thirds of each line; they now line up with the ruler and the features above them.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.5.2, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  The database gains one change: the Hazard field of Chemicals and Reagents becomes several-choice (migration 0030); a backup is taken first. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
