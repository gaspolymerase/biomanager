## BioManager 2.0.0

### Works differently

- **Sheets open much faster.** A long sheet used to keep the browser busy for seconds before you could use it: every row was drawn, and every row's fields were looked through, before the page appeared. Now the page you see is drawn first and the rest as you turn to it. In a lab of 4,000 mice, in Chrome: the mouse sheet opens in 2.7 s instead of 6.2 s, the cage sheet in 3.1 s instead of 8.0 s, the litters in 1.5 s instead of 4.0 s, an inventory in under 3 s instead of 5 to 6 s. The bigger the sheet, the bigger the difference: in a colony of thousands of living mice the mouse sheet took over a minute in Chrome, and now about 8 s. Nothing to change in how you work: click a cell and type, as before.
  - **表格打开快多了。** 以前打开一张长表格时，浏览器要先把每一行画出来、把每一行的字段都过一遍，要忙上好几秒才能用。现在先画你看到的这一页，其余的翻到时再画。在一个有 4,000 只小鼠的实验室里，用 Chrome：小鼠表从 6.2 秒变成 2.7 秒，笼子表从 8.0 秒变成 3.1 秒，窝表从 4.0 秒变成 1.5 秒，库存表从 5 到 6 秒变成不到 3 秒。表格越长，差别越大：在有几千只活小鼠的鼠群里，小鼠表在 Chrome 里要一分多钟，现在大约 8 秒。用法不变：点一个单元格直接输入，和以前一样。
- **The fish sheet** took over 30 s to open in a lab with a hundred tanks, and longer the more tanks it had: each row carried the lab's whole list of tanks. Each row now holds its own tank, and the list comes when you open the **Tank** menu. It opens in about 3 s.
  - **鱼表** 在有一百个鱼缸的实验室里要 30 多秒才打开，鱼缸越多越慢：每一行都带着全实验室的鱼缸列表。现在每一行只带它自己的鱼缸，打开 **鱼缸** 菜单时才载入列表。大约 3 秒就能打开。
- **Searching a long sheet** keeps up with your typing: each row's text is read once, not again on every key.
  - **在长表格里搜索** 能跟上你打字的速度：每一行的文字只读一次，不再每按一个键都重读。
- **The calendar** fills its month faster: the sac reminders and the parents of each litter are looked up once, not once a mouse.
  - **日历** 填满一个月更快了：处死提醒和每窝的父母只查一次，不再每只小鼠查一次。
- **Pages ask a lab server for less.** The browser keeps the app's scripts and styles until a new version replaces them, rather than asking for each again on every page.
  - **页面向实验室服务器要的东西更少。** 浏览器会留着应用的脚本和样式，直到新版本替换它们，不再每打开一页都重新要一遍。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.0.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database's tables. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
