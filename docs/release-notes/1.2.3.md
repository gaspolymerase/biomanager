## BioManager 1.2.3

### New

- **Settings is laid out like the Mac's System Settings**: a list of panes on the left, the chosen one beside it, each setting in a row with a short line on what it does, and a search box. A change is saved as you make it.
  - **You**: Profile, Sign-in & security, Appearance & language, Notifications, AI assistant & tokens, Your data.
  - **Lab**: Statistics, General, Databases, People & access, Devices & copies, History. Everyone sees them; admins change them.
- **Statistics**: the lab's mice, fish tanks and fly and worm vials, by person; how full each rack and box is, and who may change it; what is coming up.
- **People & access** holds the lab's people in one place: sign-ups waiting to join, members (role, password and disabling under the **···** beside each one), guests, project groups, and what members may do.
- **What members may do in a project group**: each person is a **Lead**, a **Member** or **Can only view**, and the group's switches say whether its members may edit what is shared with it, add and change records in its databases, edit each other's records there, edit its notebook pages, tick off its to-dos, and share their own things with it. They start as groups have always worked.
- **Home is built from the lab's own databases**, in the sidebar's order: each fly, worm and animal database has a card of its own, **Counts** has a tile per database, and there is a **Recent plasmids** card.

### What works differently

- The sidebar's **More** menu is gone: Lab setup, Manage users, Guests, Project groups, Colony overview, Racks & boxes and Batch history are panes in **Settings** now, and their old links open the pane.
- The **Genotyping** dialog says to type the parents' mouse numbers and how many pups were born.
- The rack **Position** column is headed **Pos** and is narrower, on the Mice and Cages tabs and fish tanks; hovering it says what it is.
- The sidebar calls Plasmids **Molecular biology** once primers, glycerol stocks or viruses sit beside it.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.2.3, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and a copy is taken before the database is upgraded.
- **Lab server**:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.

  The database upgrades itself on start, adding the project groups' switches and each member's part; every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
