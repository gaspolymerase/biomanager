## BioManager 2.4.0

### New

- **Any icon for a notebook topic.** A topic's **Name and icon** now has **Icons · Emoji · SVG**: BioManager's own icons, the lab's usual emoji, any emoji at all under **Any emoji** (your computer's emoji panel: Control+⌘+Space on a Mac, Windows key + . on Windows), or **Upload an SVG…** of your own, up to 100 KB, such as your lab's logo. Scripts and links in an SVG are taken out, and it is kept with the lab's other files, in its backups.
  - **实验记录本的主题可以用任何图标。** 主题的 **名称和图标** 里现在有 **图标 · 表情 · SVG**：BioManager 自带的图标、实验室常用的表情、在 **其他表情** 里输入的任意表情（电脑自带的表情面板：Mac 上是 Control+⌘+空格，Windows 上是 Windows 键 + .），或者 **上传 SVG…** 一张自己的图，最大 100 KB，比如实验室的标志。SVG 里的脚本和链接会被去掉，它和实验室的其他文件一起保存，也在备份里。

### Works differently

- **The notebook has one sidebar.** In the notebook, BioManager's own sidebar folds to its icons (**»** at its top opens it for a moment), and the notebook's sidebar runs the window's height. At its top, your notebook's name opens **Home**, **+** makes a **New page** in the topic you are in, and **Find a page…** narrows the list as you type (Enter searches inside every page). **Today**, **Protocols**, **Recipes**, **Meetings** and **Reading** stay at its foot.
  - **实验记录本只有一个侧边栏。** 进入记录本时，BioManager 自己的侧边栏收成一列图标（点它顶部的 **»** 可以暂时展开），记录本的侧边栏从窗口顶部一直到底。顶部是你的记录本名称，点它打开 **首页**；**+** 在当前主题里 **新建页面**；**查找页面…** 边输入边筛选列表（按回车在所有页面里搜索）。**今天**、**实验流程**、**配方**、**会议** 和 **文献** 固定在底部。
- **Your round badge shows your short name** (**Settings → Short name**, up to 5 letters, THX say), or your initials when you haven't set one.
  - **圆形头像标记显示你的简称**（**设置 → 简称**，最多 5 个字母，比如 THX）；没设简称时显示姓名首字母。
- **Easier to read.** Grey and coloured text is darker in light mode and lighter in dark mode, so every label and hint reads clearly against its background.
  - **更容易看清。** 灰色和彩色的文字在浅色模式下更深、在深色模式下更浅，每个标签和提示在背景上都清楚可读。

### Fixed

- **On Linux, a computer without working graphics** (a virtual machine, remote desktop, a server's screen) now opens BioManager's window, drawn without the graphics card, instead of stopping. It does so by itself when the window didn't open last time.
  - **在 Linux 上，没有可用显卡加速的电脑**（虚拟机、远程桌面、服务器的屏幕）现在也能打开 BioManager 的窗口（不用显卡绘制），不会再直接退出。上次窗口没打开时，它会自动这样做。
- **On a phone**, a notebook page's title is full size again.
  - **在手机上**，实验记录本页面的标题恢复了正常大小。
- **Screen readers** read the window's tabs, Home's views and the Calendar's views as the buttons and links they are.
  - **读屏软件** 会把窗口的标签、首页的视图和日历的视图正确读作按钮和链接。

### Opening it the first time on macOS 15

macOS 15 no longer lets right-click → **Open** past the warning for an app Apple hasn't checked. Open BioManager, choose **Done** at the warning, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and choose **Open Anyway**. It asks once per version.

macOS 15 起，右键 → **打开** 已经不能绕过未经 Apple 检查的应用的警告。打开 BioManager，在警告里选 **完成**，再到 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性**，往下滚动，选 **仍要打开**。每个版本只问一次。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.4.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes in this version. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
