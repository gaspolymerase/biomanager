## BioManager 2.0.7

### New

- **Set strain, sex, litter or date of birth on many mice at once.** Tick mice on **Mice** and the bar's **Set** now offers **Strain**, **Sex**, **Litter** and **Date of birth** beside Owner, Status, Transgenes, Cage, Note and Date of death, in the order the sheet shows them. The box for the value follows what you pick: a date picker for a date, and the field's own choices (your strains, the sexes, statuses, people, a column's options) offered as you type. **Sex** takes only the sheet's choices. A date of birth belongs to the litter: tick every mouse of a litter and the litter itself takes the new date, keeping its parents; mice ticked from part of a litter move together into a new litter of that date. Leaving the box empty asks first, and **Settings → History** undoes any of it.
  - **一次为多只小鼠设置品系、性别、窝或出生日期。** 在 **小鼠** 里勾选小鼠后，底部栏的 **设置** 除了负责人、状态、转基因、笼、备注和死亡日期，现在还可以选 **品系**、**性别**、**窝** 和 **出生日期**，顺序和表格里的列一致。填值的框会跟着所选的字段变：日期给出日期选择器，其他字段在输入时列出它自己的选项（实验室的品系、性别、状态、成员、自定义列的选项）。**性别** 只接受表格里的选项。出生日期属于窝：勾选一窝的全部小鼠时，改的是这窝本身的日期，亲本信息保留；只勾选了一窝中的几只，它们会一起移到一个新窝，日期为所填的日期。值留空时会先询问，所有改动都可以在 **设置 → 历史** 里撤销。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.0.7, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database's tables. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
