## BioManager 1.2.8

### New

- **Write notebook pages as in Notion.** Type **/** and a few letters of what you want; Chinese words find things too (`/分栏`, `/折叠`, `/提醒`).
  - Headings, lists, checklists, quotes and dividers.
  - **Callouts**: a coloured box (note, tip, important, warning, caution) for what nobody should miss.
  - **Toggles**: a line that opens to show what it hides. Opening one opens it only for you.
  - **2 or 3 columns** side by side, such as a gel picture and its notes; on a phone they stack.
  - **Colour and highlight** text (the **A** on the toolbar), a **link to another page**, a **table of contents**, a date, and a **reminder** that puts a to-do in your calendar and links to it.
  - Pages stay readable Markdown, so an exported page reads anywhere.
- **@ a colleague** and their name shows in their colour. Hover it to see who they are: name, job, admin, member or guest, email, project groups, and how long they've been in the lab.
- **Utilities, redesigned.**
  - A search that knows the bench's words (c1v1, nanodrop, × g, 稀释).
  - The tools you pinned and opened last.
  - Seven groups by bench task.
  - Each tool opens on a worked example with its answer.
  - New tools: restriction digests, split & passage, concentrate & dialysis, a constant-DMSO dose series, lentivirus packaging, pouring plates, a dosing sheet for many animals, ΔΔCt from pasted Cts, equal RNA input, and the lab's saved rotors for rpm ↔ × g.
- **The lab's own tools.** A calculation Utilities doesn't have can be made a tool: **Make a tool** on *The lab's own* card, with inputs and answers written as formulas. Everyone in the lab can use it.

### Works differently

- The notebook's bottom toolbar fits in one row:
  - **Style** holds Text, Heading 1–3, Quote and Code block.
  - **Lists** holds bulleted, numbered and checklist.
  - One button adds a picture or a file.
  - The data sheet and time stamp are in **Insert** and the **/** menu; the time stamp is still Ctrl+Shift+;.
  - On a narrow screen the bar scrolls sideways.

### Fixed

- A BCA standard curve with replicate readings (`125 0.21 0.22`) read the second number as a reading of its own. Replicates are now averaged, and ones more than 10 % apart are flagged.
- Protein from A₂₈₀ now says when a typed ε or MW is used instead of the sequence's.
- Penicillin–streptomycin is no longer suggested for agar.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.2.8, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes from 1.2.7. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
