## BioManager 2.5.0

### New

- **Plasmids, drawn by the app.** Open a plasmid and its tabs show it:
  - **Overview**: the map, saved as SVG or PNG.
  - **Sequence**: drag across bases to make a primer; a coding feature shows its protein.
  - **Digest**: the gel your enzymes would give, Dam/Dcm methylation included, and a **Suggested check**.
  - **Verify**: each clone with its sequencing traces; **Accept** the one that matches.
  - **Primers**: the lab's primers that bind, and the **PCR** product from any two.

  The plasmid list shows a small map of each.
  - **质粒由软件直接画出来。** 打开一个质粒，各个标签页会显示：
    - **概览**：质粒图谱，可以存成 SVG 或 PNG。
    - **序列**：拖选碱基就能设计引物；编码区会显示它的蛋白序列。
    - **酶切**：所选酶切出来的胶图，考虑 Dam/Dcm 甲基化，并给出 **推荐检测**。
    - **核对**：每个克隆和它的测序峰图；**接受** 与之相符的那个。
    - **引物**：实验室里能结合上的引物，以及任意两条做出的 **PCR** 产物。

    质粒列表里每个质粒都显示一张小图谱。
- **Saved views on every sheet.** Filter a sheet, then **Save view** to come back to it; filters show as chips you can take off one by one. Select rows and a bar offers what you can do to all of them, and **Set a field…** now works on every column.
  - **每张表格都能保存视图。** 筛选表格后点 **保存视图**，以后可以直接回到这里；筛选条件显示成可以逐个去掉的小标签。选中多行时会出现一条操作栏，**设置字段…** 现在对每一列都有效。
- **Share a notebook topic** with lab mates: they find it under **Shared with me**.
  - **实验记录本的主题可以共享** 给实验室同事，他们在 **与我共享** 里找到。
- **Search from anywhere** with ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux): jump to a page, a record or an action.
  - 在任何地方按 ⌘K（Windows 和 Linux 上是 Ctrl+K）**搜索**：直接跳到某个页面、某条记录或某个操作。

### Works differently

- **A new look across the app**: a glass sidebar (hover over a database to look inside it), new icons and type, and your lab's own icon and name at the top of the sidebar.
  - **全新的界面**：半透明的侧边栏（鼠标停在某个数据库上可以预览里面的内容）、新的图标和字体，侧边栏顶部是实验室自己的图标和名称。
- **Home** opens on a **Daily brief** of what is due and what changed.
  - **首页** 先显示 **今日简报**：今天要做的事和最近的变化。
- **Meetings live in the calendar**, and the calendar's **Board** keeps to-dos; write a day on a card and it shows on that day.
  - **会议移到了日历里**，日历的 **看板** 用来放待办；在卡片上写上日期，它就会出现在那一天。
- **Protocols** gathers every protocol in one place.
  - **实验流程** 把所有实验流程集中在一处。
- **Dropdowns** are redesigned, and the colony's pages lose their doubled buttons.
  - **下拉菜单** 重新设计了，鼠群页面上重复的按钮也去掉了。

### Fixed

- **On a lab server reached over Tailscale**, HTTPS no longer stops working after Tailscale on the server restarts (after an update, say). Before, phones and browsers said they *couldn't establish a secure connection* until someone restarted Caddy.
  - **通过 Tailscale 访问的实验室服务器**，在服务器上的 Tailscale 重启后（比如更新之后），HTTPS 不会再失效。以前手机和浏览器会提示 *无法建立安全连接*，要等有人重启 Caddy 才恢复。
- **A sheet keeps its place** after a change to many rows at once.
  - 一次修改多行之后，**表格停在原来的位置**。

### Opening it the first time on macOS 15

macOS 15 no longer lets right-click → **Open** past the warning for an app Apple hasn't checked. Open BioManager, choose **Done** at the warning, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and choose **Open Anyway**. It asks once per version.

macOS 15 起，右键 → **打开** 已经不能绕过未经 Apple 检查的应用的警告。打开 BioManager，在警告里选 **完成**，再到 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性**，往下滚动，选 **仍要打开**。每个版本只问一次。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.5.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and a copy is taken before the database is upgraded.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time and, with Tailscale, keeps HTTPS working when Tailscale restarts.

  The database upgrades itself on start (the log says `Upgrading the database from … to …`); every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
