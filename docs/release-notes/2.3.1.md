## BioManager 2.3.1

### Fixed

- **Mac Calendar: an event across the night the clocks change.** On macOS 14, an event in BioManager that runs across the night the clocks go back (overnight imaging from Oct 31 to Nov 1, say) showed in the Mac's Calendar ending an hour late, and was taken out and put back at every update. It now keeps its times, on macOS 14 and 15; events already there are put right at the first update.
  - **Mac 日历：跨过调时那一夜的活动。** 在 macOS 14 上，BioManager 里跨过时钟回拨那一夜的活动（比如 10 月 31 日到 11 月 1 日的通宵成像），在 Mac 日历里显示的结束时间晚了一小时，而且每次更新都会被删掉再重新放进去。现在它的时间保持不变，macOS 14 和 15 都是如此；已经在日历里的活动会在第一次更新时改正。
- **Mac Calendar calls the Mac's own account On My Mac**, as the Mac's Calendar does, not "Default".
  - **Mac 日历把 Mac 自己的账户叫作“我的 Mac”**，和 Mac 的日历一样，不再显示为 “Default”。
- **On Linux, when BioManager's window can't open** (a computer without the graphics libraries it needs), it opens in your web browser instead of stopping, as on Windows. Leave its message open while you use it: **OK** closes BioManager.
  - **在 Linux 上，BioManager 的窗口打不开时**（电脑缺少它需要的图形库），会改在浏览器里打开，而不是直接退出，和 Windows 上一样。使用期间请不要关掉它的提示窗口：点 **确定** 会关闭 BioManager。

### Opening it the first time on macOS 15

macOS 15 no longer lets right-click → **Open** past the warning for an app Apple hasn't checked. Open BioManager, choose **Done** at the warning, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and choose **Open Anyway**. It asks once per version.

macOS 15 起，右键 → **打开** 已经不能绕过未经 Apple 检查的应用的警告。打开 BioManager，在警告里选 **完成**，再到 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性**，往下滚动，选 **仍要打开**。每个版本只问一次。

### Updating

- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 2.3.1, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  Nothing in the database changes in this version. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
