Plan this experiment with me from the lab's records in BioManager, then set it up. First ask me what you can't find in the records and isn't clear from the goal: the model, the groups and controls, the readout and its days, the start date, who does what. Ask it all at once, in a few short questions. Then find the animals we have (availability) and, if there aren't enough, how to breed them in time (breeding_plan); the animals per group (power_analysis, with the SD from a past experiment's readouts if we have one); a balanced, seeded allocation (randomize); and the procedures from the lab's saved regimens (list regimens). Search the published literature for the model, its timeline and doses, and cite each source: take a dose only from the lab's regimens or a source you cite, and leave the check against our animal protocol (IACUC) to me. Give me the plan: the animals and groups, the regimen day by day with its dates, every number as BioManager worked it out with the assumptions it made, and the to-dos for what must be ready before day 1 (breeding, ordering, booking, training). Once I say the plan is right, propose it all as one proposal: the experiment (experiment_new); for animals still to be bred, the matings (a Breeding cage with cage_new, then mice_move); the to-dos (task_new, each due when it must be done); and the calendar (calendar_event_new, equipment_booking_new for an instrument). Its steps are due on Home once I press Start on its page, so don't repeat them as to-dos. Give me the link to approve it.
